Bangladesh was represented at the [[1996 Summer Olympics]] in [[Atlanta]], Georgia, United States by the [[Bangladesh Olympic Association]].

In total, four athletes including three men and one woman represented Bangladesh in three different sports including athletics, shooting and swimming.

==Athletics==
{{main|Athletics at the 1996 Summer Olympics}}

In total, two Bangladeshi athletes participated in the athletics events – [[Bimal Tarafdar]] in the [[Athletics at the 1996 Summer Olympics – Men's 100 metres|men's 100&nbsp;m]] and [[Nilufar Yasmin (athlete)|Nilufar Yasmin]] in the [[Athletics at the 1996 Summer Olympics – Women's long jump|women's long Jump]].<ref name="bangladesh"/>

The heats for the men's 100&nbsp;m took place on 26 July 1996. Tarafdar finished seventh in his heat in a time of 10.98 seconds. He failed to advance to the quarter-finals.<ref>{{cite web |title=1996 Summer Olympics, 100 meters, Men |url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/64629 |publisher=Olympedia |access-date=16 June 2025}}</ref>

Qualifying for the women's long jump took place on 1 August 1996. On her first attempt, Yasmin jumped 5.19&nbsp;m. She did not improve on her second attempt (4.65&nbsp;m) but her third attempt was her best at 5.24&nbsp;m. She did not advance to the finals and finished 36th overall.<ref>{{cite web |title=1996 Summer Olympics, Long Jump, Women |url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/65179 |publisher=Olympedia |access-date=16 June 2025}}</ref>

==Shooting==
{{main|Shooting at the 1996 Summer Olympics}}

In total, one Bangladeshi athlete participated in the shooting events – [[Saiful Alam (shooter)|Saiful Alam]] in the [[Shooting at the 1996 Summer Olympics – Men's 10 metre air rifle|men's 10&nbsp;m air rifle]].<ref name="bangladesh"/>

The men's 10&nbsp;m air rifle took place on 22 July 1996. Alam scored 581 points in the preliminary round. He did not advance to the final round and finished joint 33rd overall.<ref>{{cite web |title=1996 Summer Olympics, Air Rifle, 10 metres, Men |url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/51884 |publisher=Olympedia |access-date=16 June 2025}}</ref>

==Swimming==
{{main|Swimming at the 1996 Summer Olympics}}

In total, one Bangladeshi athlete participated in the swimming events – [[Karar Rahman]] in the [[Swimming at the 1996 Summer Olympics – Men's 100 metre breaststroke|men's 100&nbsp;m breaststroke]].<ref name="bangladesh"/>

The heats for the men's 100&nbsp;m breaststroke took place on 20 July 1996. Rahman finished fourth in his heat in a time of one minute 11.47 seconds which was ultimately not fast enough to qualify for the finals.<ref>{{cite web |title=1996 Summer Olympics, 100 metres Breaststroke, Men |url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/8044 |publisher=Olympedia |access-date=16 June 2025}}</ref>
