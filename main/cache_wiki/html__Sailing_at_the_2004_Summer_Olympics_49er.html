The Mixed 49er was a sailing event on the Sailing at the 2004 Summer Olympics program in Agios Kosmas Olympic Sailing Centre.[1] sixteen races were scheduled and completed with two discards. 38 sailors, on 19 boats, from 19 nation competed.[2][3]
