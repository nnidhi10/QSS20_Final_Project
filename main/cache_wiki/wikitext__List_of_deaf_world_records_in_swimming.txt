The '''Deaf world records in swimming''' are the fastest ever performances of [[deaf]] athletes, which are recognised and ratified by the [[Comité International des Sports des Sourds|International Committee of Sports for the Deaf]] (ICSD) and [[FINA]].

All records were set in finals unless noted otherwise.
