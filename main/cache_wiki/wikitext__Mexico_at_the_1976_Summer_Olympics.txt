Mexico competed at the [[1976 Summer Olympics]] in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. 97 competitors, 92 men and 5 women, took part in 54 events in 17 sports.<ref name="sports-reference">{{cite web |url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/countries/MEX/summer/1976/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200417052336/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/countries/MEX/summer/1976/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=17 April 2020 |title=Mexico at the 1976 Montreal Summer Games |access-date=17 April 2015 |work=sports-reference.com}}</ref>
