'''Hryhoriy Anatoliyovych Misyutin''' ([[Ukrainian language|Ukrainian]]: Григорій Анатолійович Місютін; born 29 December 1970) is a Ukrainian [[artistic gymnast]], formerly representing the Soviet Union and the Unified Team. At the [[1992 Summer Olympics]], he won a gold medal in the team event and four individual silver medals. He is the [[1991 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1991 World]] all-around, rings, and team champion. He is also the [[1992 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1992 World]] horizontal bar champion, the [[1993 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1993 World]] floor exercise champion, and the [[1995 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1995 World]] vault champion. He helped Ukraine win the team bronze medal at the [[1996 Summer Olympics]].

==Gymnastics career==
At the [[1991 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1991 World Championships]], Misyutin helped the Soviet Union win its fourth straight World team title. He also qualified for the all-around final in second place, behind teammate [[Vitaly Scherbo]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Horner |first1=Scott |title=Soviet men win gold at World Gymnastics |url=https://www.upi.com/Archives/1991/09/10/Soviet-men-win-gold-at-World-Gymnastics/8512684475200/ |website=[[United Press International]] |access-date=6 September 2025 |date=10 September 1991}}</ref> He went on to win the all-around title ahead of Scherbo,<ref>{{cite web |title=Soviets sweep World Gymnastics all-around |url=https://www.upi.com/Archives/1991/09/12/Soviets-sweep-World-Gymnastics-all-around/8113684648000/ |website=[[United Press International]] |last1=Horner |first1=Scott |access-date=6 September 2025 |date=12 September 1991}}</ref> and he won another gold medal on the rings.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Horner |first1=Scott |title=North Korean wins uneven bars with 10 at World Gymnastics |url=https://www.upi.com/Archives/1991/09/14/North-Korean-wins-uneven-bars-with-10-at-World-Gymnastics/1813684820800/ |website=[[United Press International]] |access-date=6 September 2025 |date=14 September 1991}}</ref> At the [[1992 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1992 World Championships]], he won a gold medal on the horizontal bar and a bronze medal on the rings.<ref>{{cite web |title=Zmeskal wins balance beam gold |url=https://www.upi.com/Archives/1992/04/19/Zmeskal-wins-balance-beam-gold/6413703656000/ |website=[[United Press International]] |access-date=6 September 2025 |date=19 April 1992}}</ref>

Misyutin won a gold medal alongside the Unified Team at the [[1992 Summer Olympics]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Barcelona 1992 Gymnastics Artistic team competition men Results |url=https://www.olympics.com/en/olympic-games/barcelona-1992/results/gymnastics-artistic/team-competition-men |website=[[International Olympic Committee]] |access-date=6 September 2025}}</ref> He then won the silver medal in the all-around final, behind Scherbo.<ref>{{cite web |title=Individual All-Around, Men |url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/70629 |website=[[Olympedia]] |access-date=6 September 2025}}</ref> In the floor exercise final, he tied with [[Yukio Iketani]] for the silver medal,<ref>{{cite web |title=Floor Exercise, Men |url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/70651 |website=[[Olympedia]] |access-date=6 September 2025}}</ref> and he won the vault silver medal behind Scherbo.<ref>{{cite web |title=Horse Vault, Men |url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/70653 |website=[[Olympedia]] |access-date=6 September 2025}}</ref> He tied with [[Andreas Wecker]] for the silver medal on the horizontal bar.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Eskenazi |first1=Gerald |title=On Scherbo's Night, Dimas Also Sparkles |url=https://www.nytimes.com/1992/08/03/sports/barcelona-gymnastics-on-scherbo-s-night-dimas-also-sparkles.html |work=The New York Times |date=3 August 1992 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20240723080007/https://www.nytimes.com/1992/08/03/sports/barcelona-gymnastics-on-scherbo-s-night-dimas-also-sparkles.html |archive-date=23 July 2024}}</ref>

At the [[1993 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1993 World Championships]], Misyutin won the floor exercise title.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Phillips |first1=Mitch |title=Miller earns gold, Scherbo disappoints |url=https://www.upi.com/Archives/1993/04/17/Miller-earns-gold-Scherbo-disappoints/5056735019200/ |website=[[United Press International]] |access-date=6 September 2025 |date=17 April 1993}}</ref> He won three gold medals at the [[1994 Goodwill Games]]– on the floor exercise, pommel horse, and vault.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Gymn Forum: 1994 Goodwill Games, Men's EF |url=https://www.gymn-forum.net/Results/GGames/Men/1994_ef.html |access-date=2022-11-19 |website=Gymn Forum}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last1=Possehl |first1=Suzanne |title=Urmanov Helps to Pave Golden Path for Russia |url=https://www.nytimes.com/1994/08/06/sports/goodwill-games-urmanov-helps-to-pave-golden-path-for-russia.html |website=[[The New York Times]] |access-date=29 July 2025 |date=6 August 1994}}</ref> He tied with Russia's [[Alexei Nemov]] for the vault title at the [[1995 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1995 World Championships]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Moceanu Captures Silver in World Gymnastics |url=https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1995-10-11-sp-55600-story.html |website=[[Los Angeles Times]] |access-date=9 September 2025 |date=11 October 1995}}</ref> There, he also won a bronze medal on the floor exercise.<ref>{{cite web |title=31st World Championships ‐ Artistic Gymnastics Sabae (JAP) October 1 ‐ 10 1995 |url=https://static.usagym.org/PDFs/Results/worlds_artistic_results_1995.pdf |website=[[USA Gymnastics]] |access-date=9 September 2025}}</ref>

Misyutin won a bronze medal on the floor exercise at the [[1996 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1996 World Championships]], behind [[Vitaly Scherbo]] and [[Aleksey Voropayev]].<ref>{{cite web |title=1996 Individual World Championships Finals |url=https://usagym.org/1996-individual-world-championships-finals/ |website=[[USA Gymnastics]] |access-date=9 September 2025 |date=19 April 1996}}</ref> He then represented [[Ukraine at the 1996 Summer Olympics]] and helped the team win the bronze medal by less than a point ahead of Belarus.<ref>{{cite web |title=Team All-Around, Men |url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/70702 |website=[[Olympedia]] |access-date=9 September 2025}}</ref> Individually, he advanced to the floor exercise final, but he placed last after making mistakes.<ref>{{cite web |title=Floor Exercise, Men |url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/70709 |website=[[Olympedia]] |access-date=9 September 2025}}</ref> After the 1996 Olympics, he retired and, in 2004, moved to [[Saarland]], Germany, to coach.<ref name="olympedia">{{cite web |title=Hryhoriy Misiutin |url=https://www.olympedia.org/athletes/31231 |website=[[Olympedia]] |access-date=6 September 2025}}</ref>
