==Day-by-day summaries==
===6 August===
'''[[Football at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Football]]'''
* The first competitions of the Games started at 5:00pm [[China Standard Time|CST]] ([[UTC+8]]). The first events were women's football (soccer) matches. The day was devoted to women's football.<ref name="Official Schedule August 6">{{cite web |url=http://results.beijing2008.cn/WRM/ENG/Schedule/FB_2008-08-06.shtml |title=Schedules & Results > Football > Wed Aug 6 2008 |date= |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date= |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080809004913/http://results.beijing2008.cn/WRM/ENG/Schedule/FB_2008-08-06.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-09}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/football/n214504690.shtml |title=Women's Football Day 1 Preview: Mouthwatering matches expected |date=2008-08-05 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date= |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080812143334/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/football/n214504690.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-12}}</ref>

===7 August===
'''[[Football at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Football]]'''
* The first football (soccer) matches for men began on this day. The day was devoted to men's football matches.<ref name="Official Schedule August 7">{{cite web |url=http://results.beijing2008.cn/WRM/ENG/Schedule/FB_2008-08-07.shtml |title=Schedules & Results > Football > Thu Aug 7 2008 |access-date= |publisher=Beijing 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080809010837/http://results.beijing2008.cn/WRM/ENG/Schedule/FB_2008-08-07.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-09}}</ref><ref>The Observer, Sunday July 27, 2008, [https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2008/jul/27/olympicgames20083 Stars join Beijing gold rush but Brits miss the party], Jamie Jackson.</ref>

===8 August===
[[Image:2008 Summer Olympics Opening Ceremony 2.jpg|thumb|250px|Fireworks during the opening ceremony]]
{{Wikinews|2008 Olympic Games officially open}}
; [[2008 Summer Olympics Opening Ceremony|Opening Ceremony]]: Starting at 8:00 pm [[China Standard Time|CST]] ([[UTC+8]]),<ref>{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/ceremonies/n214508163.shtml |title=Opening Ceremony plan released |date=2008-08-06 |access-date=2008-08-10 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080912045614/http://en.beijing2008.cn/ceremonies/n214508163.shtml |archive-date=2008-09-12}}</ref> the four-hour opening ceremony was attended by 91,000 spectators,<ref>{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/ceremonies/headlines/n214519367.shtml |title=Beijing welcomes world to 2008 Olympic Games |date=2008-08-09 |access-date=2008-08-10 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090428172510/http://en.beijing2008.cn/ceremonies/headlines/n214519367.shtml |archive-date=2009-04-28 |url-status=dead}}</ref> including more than 100 world leaders. The spectacle was directed by filmmaker [[Zhang Yimou]], and the [[Olympic Flame#Cauldron|Olympic Cauldron]] was lit by former Chinese gymnast [[Li Ning]].<ref>{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/ceremonies/photos/openingceremony/n214518986.shtml |title=Former gymnast Li Ning lights Olympic cauldron |date=2008-08-09 |access-date=2008-08-10 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080809000906/http://en.beijing2008.cn/ceremonies/photos/openingceremony/n214518986.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-09 |url-status=dead}}</ref> <!--(The Olympic cauldron was the last to be lit outdoors until the [[2012 Summer Olympics]]. ===Day 1: 9 August===
{{Wikinews|Olympic highlights: August 9, 2008}}

'''[[Archery at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Archery]]'''
* [[South Korea at the 2008 Summer Olympics|South Korea]] set an [[List of Olympic records in archery|Olympic record]] in the ranking round of the [[Archery at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's team|women's team archery]] event.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/archery/n214522492.shtml |title=Archery Day 1 Review: Republic of Korea women break Olympic record |date=2008-08-09 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-09 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080812063333/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/archery/n214522492.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-12}}</ref>
'''[[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Boxing]]'''
* [[Samoa at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Samoa]]'s [[Satupaitea Farani Tavui|Farani Tavui]] was taken to a hospital after being knocked unconscious in the [[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Light heavyweight|men's light heavyweight]] event.<ref>[http://sportsillustrated.cnn.com/2008/olympics/2008/08/09/boxer.injured.ap/?eref=sircrc "Samoan boxer injured in knockout"]{{dead link|date=September 2024|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}, Associated Press, August 9, 2008.</ref>
'''[[Fencing at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Fencing]]'''
* The [[United States at the 2008 Summer Olympics|United States]] took all three medals in the [[Fencing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's sabre|women's sabre]] event, the first U.S. podium sweep of a fencing event since 1904, as [[Mariel Zagunis]] takes gold.<ref name="women's sabre">{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/fencing/n214522613.shtml |title=United States makes Fencing history |date=2008-08-09 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080812033231/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/fencing/n214522613.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-12 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
'''[[Judo at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Judo]]'''
* [[Choi Min-ho (judoka)|Choi Min-ho]] of [[South Korea at the 2008 Summer Olympics|South Korea]] won the [[Judo at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 60 kg|men's 60&nbsp;kg]] ending all his matches with [[ippon]].<ref name="Judo Day 1 Review">{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/judo/n214522850.shtml |title=Judo Day 1 Review: Romania and ROK rumble to victory |date=2008-08-09 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120328142129/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/judo/n214522850.shtml |archive-date=2012-03-28 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
* [[Ryoko Tani]] of [[Japan at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Japan]], who had never lost in a major international competition since 1996, was defeated in the [[Judo at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 48 kg|women's 48&nbsp;kg]] semifinals by eventual gold medalist [[Alina Alexandra Dumitru]] with a controversial penalty point.
'''[[Shooting at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Shooting]]'''
* [[Kateřina Emmons]] of the [[Czech Republic at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Czech Republic]] won the first gold medal of the games, setting an [[List of Olympic records in shooting#Women's records|Olympic record]] for both the qualifying (with a perfect 400) and final scores, in the [[Shooting at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 10 metre air rifle|women's 10&nbsp;m air rifle]].<ref name="Kateřina Emmons">{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/shooting/n214520230.shtml |title=Emmons wins first gold medal of the Games |date=2008-08-09 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120328142324/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/shooting/n214520230.shtml |archive-date=2012-03-28 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
'''[[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Swimming]]'''
* Swimmer [[Michael Phelps]] of the United States set a [[List of Olympic records in swimming|world record]] in the first round of the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 400 metre individual medley|men's 400&nbsp;m individual medley]].<ref>{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214522203.shtml |title=Phelps rewrites Olympic mark for Men's 400m Individual Medley |date=2008-08-09 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-09 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080809234100/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214522203.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-09 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
* Swimmer [[Alexander Dale Oen]] of [[Norway at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Norway]] set an [[List of Olympic records in swimming|Olympic record]] in the first round of the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 100 metre breaststroke|men's 100&nbsp;m breaststroke]].<ref>{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214522749.shtml |title=Men's 100m Breaststroke: Five swimmers break Olympic Record |date=2008-08-09 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-09 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080812054209/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214522749.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-12 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
'''[[Weightlifting at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Weightlifting]]'''
* [[Chen Xiexia]] of [[China at the 2008 Summer Olympics|China]] won the [[Weightlifting at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 48 kg|women's 48&nbsp;kg event in weightlifting]], setting [[List of Olympic records in weightlifting#Women's records|Olympic records]] in both the [[clean and jerk]], and total weight lifted.<ref name="Chen Xiexia">{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/weightlifting/n214520710.shtml |title=Champion Chen of China with 48kg Olympic Record |date=2008-08-09 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080812032359/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/weightlifting/n214520710.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-12 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Day 2: 10 August===
{{Wikinews|Olympic highlights: August 10, 2008}}
; [[Archery at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Archery]]:
*[[South Korea at the 2008 Summer Olympics|South Korea]] set a world record for a 24-arrow team match, in their victory over Italy in the quarter finals of the [[Archery at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's team|women's team archery]] event. They went on to win the gold medal, stretching the country's winning streak to 20 years over 6 Olympic games.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://results.beijing2008.cn/WRM/ENG/INF/AR/C81/ARW000000.shtml#ARW470301 |title=Records Broken - Archery Women |access-date=2008-08-10 |date=2008-08-10 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080812085840/http://results.beijing2008.cn/WRM/ENG/INF/AR/C81/ARW000000.shtml#ARW470301 |archive-date=2008-08-12}}
</ref>
; [[Basketball at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Basketball]]:
*The USA men's basketball team beat hosts China 101–70 in the first game in the basketball event.<ref>
{{cite web |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/basketball/n214527132.shtml |title=USA breaks Chinese hearts, 100 points |access-date=2008-08-10 |date=2008-08-10 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080813041827/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/basketball/n214527132.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-13 |url-status=dead}}
</ref>
; [[Shooting at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Shooting]]:
*[[Guo Wenjun]] of [[China at the 2008 Summer Olympics|China]] won gold in [[Shooting at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 10 metre air pistol|women's 10 metre air pistol]] and set a new Olympic record for final score with 492.3 points, after [[Natalia Paderina]] of [[Russia at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Russia]] had bettered the Olympic qualification record to 391.<ref name="Guo claims Olympic record and gold in Women's 10m Air Pistol">{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/shooting/n214524276.shtml |title=Guo claims Olympic record and gold in Women's 10m Air Pistol |date=2008-08-10 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-10 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080813032122/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/shooting/n214524276.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-13}}</ref>  During the medal ceremony, Pederina and bronze medalist [[Nino Salukvadze]] of [[Georgia at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Georgia]] shared a symbolic embrace as their two countries [[2008 South Ossetia war|continued to wage war]].<ref>[https://www.bbc.co.uk/blogs/olympics/2008/08/georgian_and_russian_athletes.html "Georgian and Russian athletes share emotional embrace"], ''[[BBC News]]'', August 10, 2008.</ref>
; [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Swimming]]:
* [[Michael Phelps]] won the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 400 metre individual medley|men's 400 m individual medley]] final, with a new world record, winning his seventh career Olympic gold medal.<ref name="Phelps1">{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214524032.shtml |title=Men's 400m Individual Medley: Phelps smashes WR for Gold |date=2008-08-10 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-11 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080813020709/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214524032.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-13 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
* [[Park Tae-Hwan]] won the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 400 metre freestyle|men's 400 m freestyle]] final, becoming the first South Korean swimmer to win an Olympic gold medal for swimming.<ref name="Park Tae-hwan">{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214524138.shtml |title=Men's 400m Freestyle: Park takes Gold, and sets Asian Record |date=2008-08-10 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-11 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080813020950/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214524138.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-13 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
* Swimmer [[Alexander Dale Oen]] of [[Norway at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Norway]] set an [[List of Olympic records in swimming|Olympic record]] in the semifinals of the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 100 metre breaststroke|men's 100 m breaststroke]], his second in the event.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214524073.shtml |title=Norway's Oen rewrites OR in Men's 100m Breaststroke Semifinal |date=2008-08-10 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080928162118/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214524073.shtml |archive-date=2008-09-28 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
*Australian swimmer [[Stephanie Rice]] set a new world record in [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 400 metre individual medley|women's 400 m individual medley]], winning Australia's 400th Summer Olympics medal. Second place [[Kirsty Coventry]] of [[Zimbabwe at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Zimbabwe]] also finished below the previous world record.<ref  name="Stephanie Rice">{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214524172.shtml |title=Women's 400m Individual Medley: Rice wins gold smashes WR |date=2008-08-10 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080813020955/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214524172.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-13 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
*The [[Netherlands at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Netherlands]] team won the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 4 × 100 metre freestyle relay|women's 4 × 100 m freestyle relay final]] with a new [[Olympic record]] of 3 minutes 33.76 seconds. ===Day 3: 11 August===
{{Wikinews|Olympic highlights: August 11, 2008}}
;[[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Swimming]]
*[[Rebecca Adlington]] won the 400&nbsp;m freestyle, the first gold by a British woman since [[Great Britain at the 1960 Summer Olympics|1960]].
*[[Kirsty Coventry]] of [[Zimbabwe at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Zimbabwe]] set a new world record in the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 100 metre backstroke|women's 100 m backstroke]] semifinal with a time of 58.77.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214527950.shtml |title=Kristy Coventry slices World Record in Women's 100m Backstroke |access-date=2008-08-11 |date=2008-08-11 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080814092819/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214527950.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-14 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
*[[Kosuke Kitajima]] of [[Japan at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Japan]] won the Olympic gold medal in the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 100 metre breaststroke|men's 100 m breaststroke]], and set a world record of 58.91.<ref name="Kitajima 100 breast">{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214527928.shtml |title=Men's 100m Breaststroke: Kitajima takes gold in world record |date=2008-08-11 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-11 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080814064454/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214527928.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-14 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
*[[Arkady Vyatchanin]] of [[Russia at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Russia]] set a new Olympic record in the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 100 metre backstroke|men's 100 m backstroke]] semifinal with a time of 53.06, only for the record to be set again in the second semifinal by [[Hayden Stoeckel]] of [[Australia at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Australia]], with a time of 52.97.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214528245.shtml |title=Stoeckel backs over Olympic record |date=2008-08-11 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-11 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081014165751/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214528245.shtml |archive-date=2008-10-14 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
*The [[United States at the 2008 Summer Olympics|United States]] team broke the world record they had set in the semifinals by nearly four seconds, winning the gold medal in the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 4 × 100 metre freestyle relay|men's 4 × 100 m freestyle relay]] with a time of 3:08:24, beating France by 0.08 seconds. [[Jason Lezak]] swam the last leg in 46.06 s, the fastest relay leg in history.  Five of the eight teams in the final finished under the previous world record. Australia's [[Eamon Sullivan]] also broke the individual 100 m freestyle world record by swimming the leadoff leg in 47.24.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214528366.shtml |title=Men's 4 x 100m Freestyle Relay: USA obliterates world record |date=2008-08-11 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-11 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080814051857/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/swimming/n214528366.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-14 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

; [[Shooting at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Shooting]]
* Entering the finals of the [[Shooting at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 10 metre air rifle|men's 10 m air rifle]] in fourth place, [[Abhinav Bindra]] won the first ever individual Olympic gold medal for India,<ref name="Abhinav Bindra">{{cite web |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/shooting/n214528114.shtml |title=India's Bindra wins gold in the Men's 10m Air Rifle |access-date=2008-08-11 |date=2008-08-11 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080814052631/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/shooting/n214528114.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-14}}</ref>  and India's first gold medal in any Olympic event since [[1980 Summer Olympics|1980]].<ref>[http://www.rediff.com/sports/2008/aug/11bindra.htm "Abhinav Bindra wins 10m air rifle gold"], rediff.com, August 11, 2008.</ref>

; [[Judo at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Judo]]:
*[[Tajikistan at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Tajikistan]] won its first ever Olympic medal after [[Rasul Boqiev]] secured bronze in the [[Judo at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 73 kg|men's 73 kg]].<ref>[http://www.yomiuri.co.jp/dy/olympics/20080812TDY28305.htm "Mammadli 1st Azeri to win Olympic gold"], ''[[Daily Yomiuri]]'', August 12, 2008.</ref>

===Day 6: 14 August===
{{Wikinews|Olympic highlights: August 14, 2008}}
; [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Swimming]]:
* [[Alain Bernard]] became the first French swimmer to win the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 100 metre freestyle|men's 100 m freestyle]], and only the third to win a gold medal in an Olympic swimming event.
* The Australian team of [[Stephanie Rice]], [[Bronte Barratt]], [[Kylie Palmer]] and [[Linda Mackenzie]] won the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 4 × 200 metre freestyle relay|women's 4 × 200 m freestyle relay]], setting a new world record time of 7 minutes 44.31 seconds and giving Rice her third gold medal at her first Olympics.<ref name="Women's 4 × 200 m freestyle relay"/>

===Day 7: 15 August===
{{Wikinews|Olympic highlights: August 15, 2008}}
; [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Athletics]]:
* [[Tirunesh Dibaba]] won the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 10,000 metres|women's 10000 m]] in 29:54.66, the best time in almost 15 years and an [[African records in athletics|African]] and [[List of Olympic records in athletics|Olympic record]]. Silver medallist [[Elvan Abeylegesse]] and bronze medallist [[Shalane Flanagan]] also ran [[European records in athletics|European]] and North American records, respectively.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.iaaf.org/OLY08/news/kind=100/newsid=46668.html |title=Dibaba smashes Olympic 10,000m record as two dip under 30 minutes! |date=2008-08-15 |publisher=[[International Association of Athletics Federations|IAAF]]}}</ref>
; [[Gymnastics at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Gymnastics]]:
* [[Nastia Liukin]] of the United States won the gold medal in the [[Gymnastics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's artistic individual all-around|Women's artistic individual all-around]].  Together with [[Shawn Johnson]] taking the silver, this was the fourth time in Olympic history where a country took the top two spots in the individual all around events.
; [[Table tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Table tennis]]:
* [[Singapore at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Singapore]] qualified for the final in the women's team event, thus ensuring that the country would win its first Olympic medal, in any event, since 1960.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20080818071709/http://news.xinhuanet.com/english/2008-08/15/content_9345411.htm "Singapore ends 48-year Olympic medal drought"], Xinhua, August 15, 2008.</ref>
; [[Judo at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Judo]]:
* Down a yuko and with only 16 seconds left, world champion China's [[Tong Wen]] managed to throw defending Olympic champion Japan's [[Maki Tsukada]] for the gold in [[Judo at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's +78 kg|women's +78 kg]].
; [[Tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Tennis]]:
* [[Simon Aspelin]] and [[Thomas Johansson]] reached the men's doubles final by beating Frenchmen [[Arnaud Clément]] and [[Michaël Llodra]] in a match lasted more than four hours and ended 19–17 in the third set.

===Day 8: 16 August===
{{Wikinews|Usain Bolt sets new world record in 100m sprint}}
; General
* New Zealand experienced their most successful day at an Olympics (with two gold, one silver, and two bronze medals), beating their previous best of four bronze medals in the [[1988 Summer Olympics|1988 Seoul Olympics]].
; [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Athletics]]:
* Russia's [[Valeriy Borchin]] won gold in the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 20 kilometres walk|men's 20 km walk]] with a time of 1:19:01, edging out [[Jefferson Pérez]], the world champion since 2003, by only 14 seconds.<ref name="Men's 20 km walk">{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/athletics/n214548378.shtml |title=Russia's Borchin claims gold in Men's 20km Walk |date=2008-08-16 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-16 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080816113922/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/athletics/n214548378.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-16 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
*Jamaica's [[Usain Bolt]] won the gold medal in the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 100 metres|men's 100m]] with a [[world record]] of 9.69 seconds in a race dubbed by [[Michael Johnson (athlete)|Michael Johnson]] as "the greatest 100m performance in the history of the event".<ref name="Men's 100 m"/>
; [[Badminton at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Badminton]]:
* [[Zhang Ning]] successfully defended her [[Badminton at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's singles|women's singles]] Olympic title against top seed [[Xie Xingfang]].<ref name="Women's singles Badminton">{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/badminton/n214549134.shtml |title=A perfect day for Zhang Ning |date=2008-08-16 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-16 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080819022338/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/badminton/n214549134.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-19 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

* The British team of [[Tom James (rower)|Tom James]], [[Steve Williams (rower)|Steve Williams]], [[Pete Reed]] and  [[Andrew Triggs Hodge]] won the men's coxless fours, the third time in a row that Great Britain had won gold in this event.<ref name="Men's coxless four"/>
*New Zealand's Caroline and Georgina Evers-Swindell successfully defended their 2004 title in the women's double sculls by beating Germany by 0.01 seconds.<ref name="Women's double sculls"/>
* [[Georgeta Andrunache]] of Romania won gold in the final [[Rowing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's coxless pair|women's coxless pair]] race, her fifth career gold, and her third consecutive Olympic gold in that event.<ref name="Women's coxless pair"/>

; General:

* Russia's [[Gulnara Galkina-Samitova]] broke her own world record in winning the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 3000 metres steeplechase|women's 3000 m steeplechase]] in a time of 8 minutes 58.81 seconds.<ref name="Women's 3000 m steeplechase"/>

; [[Diving at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Diving]]:

;[[Gymnastics at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Gymnastics]]:
*[[Louis Smith (gymnast)|Louis Smith]], in winning the bronze medal in the [[Gymnastics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's pommel horse|men's pommel horse]], became the first British gymnast to win an individual apparatus medal in [[gymnastics]] and the first Briton to win any individual [[gymnastics]] medal since [[Walter Tysall]] won [[Gymnastics at the 1908 Summer Olympics – Men's all-around|men's all-around]] medal in 1908.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/olympics/gymnastics/7566647.stm |title=Smith wins historic bronze for GB |access-date=2008-08-17 |publisher=[[BBC Sport]] |date=2008-08-17}}</ref>
; [[Rowing at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Rowing]]:
* China won its first ever Olympic rowing gold medal in the [[Rowing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's quadruple sculls|women's quadruple sculls]].<ref>{{cite news |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/olympics/rowing/7566307.stm |title=First China rowing win denies GB |date=2008-08-17 |publisher=BBC Sport |access-date=2008-08-17}}</ref><ref name="Women's quadruple sculls" />
; [[Sailing at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Sailing]]:
* [[Ben Ainslie]] of Britain successfully defended his title, winning gold in the [[Sailing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Finn class|Finn class]].
; [[Shooting at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Shooting]]:
* [[Matthew Emmons]] finished fourth in the [[Shooting at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 50 metre rifle three positions|men's 50 m rifle three positions]] event having held the lead with one shot remaining: four years earlier he had fallen from first to eighth in the last shot of the event. China's [[Qiu Jian (sport shooter)|Qiu Jian]], who had entered the final in fourth position, shot the highest score in the final round to win the gold medal.<ref name="Men's 50 m rifle three positions"/>
; [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Swimming]]:
* The United States defeated Australia in the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 4 × 100 metre medley relay|men's 4 × 100 m medley relay]], setting a new world record of 3 minutes 29.34 seconds. [[Michael Phelps]], swimming the third leg, won his eighth Olympic gold medal of the Beijing Games, surpassing [[Mark Spitz]] to become the athlete to win [[List of multiple Olympic gold medalists at a single Games|the most gold medals in a single Olympiad]].<ref name="Men's 4 × 100 m medley relay"/>
* Germany's [[Britta Steffen]] won the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 50 metre freestyle|women's 50 m freestyle]] setting a new Olympic record of 24.06 seconds and beating American [[Dara Torres]] by just 0.01 seconds.<ref name="Women's 50 m freestyle"/>
* Australia won the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 4 × 100 metre medley relay|women's 4 × 100 m medley relay]] and set a new world record of 3:52.69, bettering the previous world record by 3.05 seconds. The United States won silver, also going under the previous world record and setting a new American record, while China won bronze with a new Asian record.<ref name="Women's 4 × 100 m medley relay"/>
; [[Table tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Table Tennis]]:
* China won the inaugural [[Table tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's team|women's team]] event by winning all ten of its singles and all five of its doubles matches in the tournament.
; [[Tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Tennis]]:
* [[Rafael Nadal]] of [[Spain at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Spain]] defeated Chile's [[Fernando González]] 6–3, 7-6 (7–2), 6–3 to win the [[Tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's singles|men's singles]], Spain's first ever Olympic gold medal in tennis. Gonzalez's medal was his third, making him Chile's most successful athlete at the Olympics.<ref name="Tennis men's singles">

; [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Athletics]]:
* [[Liu Xiang (hurdler)|Liu Xiang]], China's defending Olympic champion in the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 110 metres hurdles|men's 110 m hurdles]], withdrew from the competition due to an injury.<ref>
{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/athletics/n214558251.shtml |title=World gasps as Liu walks away from the hurdles |date=2008-08-18 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-18 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080821094206/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/athletics/n214558251.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-21 |url-status=dead}}

*[[Irving Saladino]] won Panama's first Olympic medal in 60 years, and their first gold medal ever in an [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's long jump|Olympic athletics]] event.<ref name="Men's long jump"/>
* The United States took all three medals in the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 400 metres hurdles|men's 400 m hurdles]], with [[Angelo Taylor]] winning gold, [[Kerron Clement]] silver and [[Bershawn Jackson]] bronze.<ref name="Men's 400 m hurdles"/>

* Like the [[Table tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's team|women]], the Chinese men won the [[Table tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's team|team competition]] without losing a match, winning all ten singles and all five doubles matches.
; [[Weightlifting at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Weightlifting]]:
* [[Andrei Aramnau]] of Belarus broke three world records, for the snatch, clean and jerk, and total, on the way to winning the [[Weightlifting at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 105 kg|men's 105 kg]] event.<ref name="Weightlifting men's 105 kg"/>

===Day 12: 20 August===
{{Wikinews|Olympic highlights: August 20, 2008}}
; [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Athletics]]:
* [[Usain Bolt]] won the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 200 metres|men's 200 m]] in a new world record time of 19.30 seconds to become the first sprinter since [[Carl Lewis]] in [[1984 Summer Olympics|1984]] to win both the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 100 metres|100 m]] and 200 m events in a single Olympiad, and the first sprinter since [[Don Quarrie]] in 1976 to hold the world records for both events simultaneously.<ref name="Men's 200 m"/>
* In the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 200 metres|men's 200 m]], United States' [[Wallace Spearmon]], who finished third in 19.85s, was disqualified for stepping out of his lane. Later, Netherlands Antilles' [[Churandy Martina]], who finished second in 19.82s, was also disqualified for stepping out of his lane.
; [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Swimming]]:
* After a race lasting nearly two hours, Russia's [[Larisa Ilchenko]] beat [[Keri-Anne Payne]] of Great Britain by 1.5 seconds to win the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's marathon 10 kilometre|women's 10 km marathon]]. Payne and fellow British swimmer [[Cassandra Patten]], who took bronze, had shared the lead for almost the entire race before being overtaken by Ilchenko with 50 metres remaining.<ref name="Women's 10 km marathon"/>
* [[Natalie du Toit]] of South Africa became the first amputee to compete in the Olympic Games since [[George Eyser]] in 1904, finishing 16th in the women's 10&nbsp;km marathon.<ref name="Women's 10 km marathon"/>
; [[Sailing at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Sailing]]:
* [[Yin Jian (windsurfer)|Yin Jian]] won China's first ever Olympic sailing gold medal in the [[Sailing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's sailboard|women's sailboard]].<ref name="Women's sailboard"/>
; [[Taekwondo at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Taekwondo]]:
* [[Rohullah Nikpai]] won Afghanistan's first Olympic medal ever by taking bronze in the [[Taekwondo at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 58 kg|men's 58 kg]] contest.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/olympics/taekwondo/7572409.stm |title=Afghans win first Olympic medal |date=2008-08-20 |publisher=BBC Sport |access-date=2008-08-21}}</ref>
* [[Wu Jingyu]] from [[China at the 2008 Summer Olympics|China]] defeated [[Thailand at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Thai]] athlete [[Buttree Puedpong]] in [[Taekwondo at the 2008 Summer Olympics|taekwondo]] [[Taekwondo at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 49 kg|women's 49 kg]] contest receiving a gold medal. Buttree became the first Thai athlete ever to win a silver medal for taekwondo and got the first silver medal for Thailand in 2008 Games.

===Day 14: 22 August===
; [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Athletics]]:
* Russia won the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 4 × 100 metres relay|women's 4 × 100 m relay]] ahead of Belgium and Nigeria,<ref name="Women's 4 × 100 m relay"/> but were disqualified on 16 August 2016 because a reanalysis of [[Yulia Chermoshanskaya]]’s samples resulted in a positive test for prohibited substances.<ref name="Women's 4 × 100 m relay 2">{{cite news |url=https://www.olympic.org/news/ioc-sanctions-yulia-chermoshanskaya-for-failing-anti-doping-test-at-beijing-2008 |title=IOC sanctions Yulia Chermoshanskaya for failing anti-doping test at Beijing 2008 |date=2016-08-16 |publisher=[[IOC]] |access-date=2016-08-17}}</ref>
* The Jamaican men's team won the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 4 × 100 metres relay|men's 4 × 100 m relay]] for the first time, setting a new world record time of 37.10 seconds. [[Usain Bolt]], running the third leg, gained his third gold medal and third world record of the Beijing games, and became the first sprinter since 1984 to win the three sprint events in a single Olympiad.<ref name="Men's 4 × 100 m relay"/><ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.iht.com/articles/ap/2008/08/22/sports/OLY-ATH-Track-and-Field.php |title=Bolt helps Jamaica smash world record in 400 relay |date=2008-08-22 |publisher=[[International Herald Tribune]] |access-date=2008-08-23}}</ref>
* [[Tirunesh Dibaba]] of Ethiopia won her second gold medal of the Beijing games, claiming the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 5000 metres|women's 5000 m]] to go with her earlier victory in the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's 10,000 metres|10000 m]].<ref name="Women's 5000 m"/>
* [[Steven Hooker]] won Australia's first gold medal in men's athletics since [[1968 Summer Olympics|1968]], in the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's pole vault|men's pole vault]].<ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.smh.com.au/news/athletics/melbourne-boy-hits-the-heights/2008/08/23/1219262545971.html |title=Hooker hits golden heights |date=2008-08-23 |publisher=[[The Sydney Morning Herald]] |access-date=2008-08-24}}</ref>
* [[Maurren Higa Maggi]] won Brazil's first ever gold medal in any women's individual event, in the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's long jump|women's long jump]].<ref>{{cite news |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/olympics/athletics/7576624.stm |title=Maggi wins Olympic long jump gold |publisher=BBC |access-date=2008-08-24 |date=2008-08-22}}</ref>
; [[Cycling at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Cycling]]:
* [[Anne-Caroline Chausson]] of France and [[Māris Štrombergs]] of Latvia took the inaugural gold medals in BMX, winning the women's and men's events respectively.<ref>
{{cite news |url=http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/cyclingbmx/n214574924.shtml |title=Cycling BMX Review: Chausson and Strombergs enjoy golden Olympic debut |date=2008-08-22 |publisher=Beijing 2008 |access-date=2008-08-24 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080824153147/http://en.beijing2008.cn/news/sports/headlines/cyclingbmx/n214574924.shtml |archive-date=2008-08-24 |url-status=dead}}
</ref>
; [[Table tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Table tennis]]:
* China won all three medals in the [[Table tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's singles|women's singles]], with [[Zhang Yining]] defending her Olympic gold medal, [[Wang Nan (table tennis)|Wang Nan]] taking silver, and [[Guo Yue (table tennis)|Guo Yue]] bronze, leaving a competitor from Singapore in fourth place for the third consecutive Olympics.<ref name="Table tennis women's singles"/>

===Day 15: 23 August===
; [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Athletics]]:
* Belgium's [[Tia Hellebaut]] won the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's high jump|women's high jump]] event, defeating the favourite and world champion [[Blanka Vlašić]] of Croatia and ending Vlasic's run of 38 wins in international competition.<ref name="Women's high jump"/><ref>{{cite news |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/olympics/athletics/7578294.stm |title=Shock high jump win for Hellebaut |date=2008-08-23 |publisher=BBC Sport |access-date=2008-08-24}}</ref>
* Sudan's [[Ismail Ahmed Ismail]] became his country's first Olympic medalist, taking silver in the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 800 metres|men's 800 metres event]].<ref name="SudanFirst">{{cite news |title=Darfur runner wins Sudan's first Olympic medal |work=World News |publisher=[[Associated Press]] |date=2008-08-24 |url=http://www.pr-inside.com/darfur-runner-wins-sudan-s-first-olympic-r770771.htm |access-date=2008-08-24 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120217035205/http://www.pr-inside.com/darfur-runner-wins-sudan-s-first-olympic-r770771.htm |archive-date=February 17, 2012}}</ref>
; [[Baseball at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Baseball]]:
* [[South Korea at the 2008 Summer Olympics|South Korea]] beat the favorite [[Cuba at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Cuba]] to win gold.
; [[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Boxing]]:
* Ukrainian [[Vasyl Lomachenko]] defeated Frenchman [[Khedafi Djelkhir]] in the first round of the gold medal fight in the [[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Featherweight|featherweight]] category, the referee stopping the fight after less than two minutes after Djelkhir received his third standing count.<ref name="Featherweight"/><ref>{{cite news |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/olympics/boxing/7578582.stm |title=Lomachenko storms to Beijing gold |date=2008-08-23 |publisher=BBC Sport |access-date=2008-08-24}}</ref>
* Thai boxer [[Somjit Jongjohor]] won a gold medal in [[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Flyweight|Flyweight]] boxing.
*Middleweight boxer [[James DeGale]] won a gold medal in the [[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Middleweight|middleweight]] class which, together with bronze medals for [[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Super heavyweight|super heavyweight]] [[David Price (boxer)|David Price]] and [[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Light heavyweight|light heavyweight]] [[Tony Jeffries]] was the best result for Great Britain in boxing since [[Great Britain at the 1956 Summer Olympics|1956]].<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2008/aug/18/olympics2008.olympicsboxing2 |title=Price knuckles down to guarantee himself a medal |access-date=2008-08-18 |work=[[The Guardian]] |date=2008-08-18 |location=London |first=Kevin |last=Mitchell}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2008/aug/19/olympics2008.olympicsboxing |title=Guaranteed bronze for light-heavyweight Jeffries |access-date=2008-08-19 |work=[[The Guardian]] |date=2008-08-19 |location=London |first=Pete |last=Nichols}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2008/aug/20/olympics2008.olympicsboxing1 |title=DeGale guarantees third boxing bronze for Britain |access-date=2008-08-20 |work=[[The Guardian]] |date=2008-08-20 |location=London |first=Kevin |last=Mitchell}}</ref>
; [[Diving at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Diving]]:
* Australia's [[Matthew Mitcham]] won the [[Diving at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 10 metre platform|men's 10 m platform]] gold medal with his final dive, preventing the Chinese team from winning every the diving event,<ref name="Men's 10 m platform"/> and achieving the highest score for an individual dive in Olympic history.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.smh.com.au/news/diving/a-perfect-10/2008/08/24/1219262633209.html |title=A perfect 10 |date=2008-08-24 |publisher=[[The Sydney Morning Herald]] |access-date=2008-08-24}}</ref>
; [[Rhythmic Gymnastics at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Rhythmic Gymnastics]]:
* [[Almudena Cid]] of Spain retired after her fourth Olympic games finals, and was the only rhythmic gymnast to compete in more than two.
*[[Evgenia Kanaeva]] of [[Russia]] finished first in every apparatus.
; [[Table tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Table tennis]]:
* The Chinese men followed the achievement of the women by taking all three medals, with [[Ma Lin (table tennis)|Ma Lin]] winning the gold, [[Wang Hao (table tennis, born 1983)|Wang Hao]] winning the silver for the second successive Olympics, and [[Wang Liqin]] taking the bronze medal in the [[Table tennis at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's singles|men's singles]]. China became the first country to win all the medals for which they are eligible in table tennis: gold, silver and bronze in the men's and women's singles, and gold in the men's and women's team tournaments.<ref name="Table tennis men's singles"/>
; [[Taekwondo at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Taekwondo]]:
* South Korea won the gold medal in the men's 80&nbsp;kg, with all four Korean athletes sent to Beijing taking gold.
* [[Ángel Matos]] of Cuba received a lifetime ban from taekwondo for assaulting the referee in the [[Taekwondo at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's +80 kg|men's +80 kg]] event. Matos had been disqualified from his bronze medal bout for taking too long to return to the action after being injured.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/olympics/taekwondo/7578743.stm |title=Matos rage overshadows Cha gold |date=2008-08-23 |publisher=BBC Sport |access-date=2008-08-24}}</ref>

===Day 16: 24 August===
; General
* With the gold medal win by [[Zhang Xiaoping]] in the [[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Light heavyweight|men's light heavyweight boxing]], China won a total of 51 gold medals at this Olympic Games, the most for any NOC since the Soviet Union won 55 in 1988.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://tucsoncitizen.com/morgue/2008/08/25/94719-the-bounce-china-s-51-golds-most-since-88-games/ |title=The Bounce: China's 51 golds most since '88 Games - Tucson Citizen Morgue, Part 1 (2006-2009) |website=Tucsoncitizen.com |access-date=10 November 2021}}</ref>

; [[Water polo at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Water polo]]:
* [[Hungary men's national water polo team|Hungary]] won a third consecutive gold medal in men's water polo .

; [[Basketball at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Basketball]]:
* The [[United States men's national basketball team|United States]] [[Basketball at the 2008 Summer Olympics - Men|men's basketball]] team defeated world champions Spain 118–107 in the final.<ref name="Men's basketball"/>
; [[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Boxing]]:
* [[Zou Shiming]] won China's first Olympic boxing gold medal, in the [[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Light flyweight|light flyweight]] category.<ref name="Light flyweight"/><ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.smh.com.au/news/boxing/shiming-fights-to-chinas-50th-gold/2008/08/24/1219516256354.html |title=Shiming fights to China's 50th gold |date=2008-08-24 |publisher=[[The Sydney Morning Herald]] |access-date=2008-08-24}}</ref>
* [[Bakhyt Sarsekbayev]] of Kazakhstan defeated Cuba's [[Carlos Banteaux Suarez]] to win the [[Boxing at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Welterweight|welterweight]] category, with the result that traditional boxing power Cuba finishes the Beijing games without any gold medals in boxing.<ref name="Welterweight"/>
; [[Handball at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Handball]]:
* France won the 302nd and last gold medal of the Beijing Olympics, beating Iceland in the final of the [[Handball at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's tournament|men's handball]].<ref name="Men's handball"/>

; [[2008 Summer Olympics closing ceremony|Closing ceremony]]
: The Closing Ceremony began at 8:00 pm [[China Standard Time]] ([[UTC+8]]). The [[8 (number)#As a lucky or unlucky number|number 8]] is associated with prosperity and confidence in [[Numbers in Chinese culture|Chinese culture]]. The Closing Ceremony concluded at 9:55 pm CST.
