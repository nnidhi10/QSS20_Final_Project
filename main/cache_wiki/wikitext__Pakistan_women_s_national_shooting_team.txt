The '''Pakistan women's national shooting team''' represents [[Pakistan]] in international [[shooting sports|shooting]] competitions. It is administered by the [[National Rifle Association of Pakistan]] (NRAP). Members of the team compete in both rifle and pistol events at competitions including the continental and regional games ([[Asian Games|Asian]] and [[South Asian Games|South Asian]] Games). In 2016, [[Minhal Sohail]] became the first  woman sports shooter from Pakistan to compete at the [[Summer Olympic Games]] when she participated on a quota place at the [[2016 Summer Olympics|Rio Games]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=First Pakistani female shooter set for Rio Olympics|url=https://www.thenews.com.pk/latest/122117-First-Pakistani-female-Minhal-Sohail-shooter-Rio-Olympics|access-date=2020-11-14|website=www.thenews.com.pk|language=en}}</ref>
