The 1935 Summer Deaflympics officially known as the 4th International Games for the Deaf was an international multi-sport event that was held from 17 August 1935 to 24 August 1935. It was hosted by London, England,[1][2] with events held at White City Stadium. It was the only parasport event to be held in the United Kingdom until the 2012 Summer Paralympics as part of the Paralympic Games.[3]

The tournament saw the debut of an American team.[4]
