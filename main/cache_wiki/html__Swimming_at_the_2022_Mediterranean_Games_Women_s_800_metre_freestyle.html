The women's 800 metre freestyle competition at the 2022 Mediterranean Games was held on 2 July 2022 at the Aquatic Center of the Olympic Complex in Bir El Djir.[1]

Records

Prior to this competition, the existing world and Mediterranean Games records were as follows:

World record  Katie Ledecky (USA) 8:04.79 Rio de Janeiro, Brazil 12 August 2016
Mediterranean Games record  Alessia Filippi (ITA) 8:20.78 Pescara, Italy 30 June 2009

The final was held at 19:15.[2]
