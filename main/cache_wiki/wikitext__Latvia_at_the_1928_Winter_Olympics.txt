Latvia participated at the [[1928 Winter Olympics]] in [[St. Moritz]], Switzerland, held between 11 and 19 February 1928. The country's participation in the Games marked its second appearance at the [[Winter Olympics]] since its debut in the [[Latvia at the 1924 Winter Olympics|previous Games]].

The Latvian team consisted of a lone athlete [[Alberts Rumba]] who competed across three events in [[speed skating]]. Rumba also served as the country's flag-bearer during the opening ceremony. Latvia did not win any medal in the Games.

== Background ==
The [[1924 Winter Olympics]] marked Latvia's first participation in the Olympic Games. After the nation made its debut in the [[Winter Olympics]] at the previous Games, this edition of the Games in [[1928 Winter Olympics|1928]] marked the nation's second appearance at the Winter Games.<ref name="Latvia">{{cite web|url=https://www.olympedia.org/countries/LAT|title=Latvia at the Olympics|work=[[Olympedia]]|access-date=1 June 2024}}</ref> 

The 1928 Winter Olympics was held in [[St. Moritz]], Switzerland, between 11 and 19 February 1928.<ref name="Olympics">{{cite web|url=https://www.olympics.com/en/olympic-games/st-moritz-1928|title=St. Moritz 1928|work=[[Olympics.com]]|access-date=3 February 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympedia.org/editions/30|title=1928 Winter Olympics|work=[[Olympedia]]|access-date=3 February 2024}}</ref> The Latvian delegation consisted of a lone athlete [[Alberts Rumba]].<ref name="Result">{{cite web|url=https://www.olympedia.org/countries/LAT/editions/30|title=Latvia at the 1928 Winter Olympics|work=[[Olympedia]]|access-date=3 February 2024}}</ref><ref name="SR">{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/countries/LAT/winter/1928/|title=Latvia at 1928 Winter Olympics|work=Sports Reference|access-date=1 June 2001|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200417092656/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/countries/LAT/winter/1928/|archive-date=17 April 2020}}</ref> Rumba also served as the country's flag-bearer in the Parade of Nations during the opening ceremony.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympedia.org/flagbearers?edition_id=30|title=Flag bearers at the 1928 Winter Olympics|work=[[Olympedia]]|access-date=3 February 2024}}</ref>  Latvia did not win any medal in the Games.<ref name="Latvia"/><ref>{{cite book|title=Résultats des Concours des II<sup>mes</sup> Jeux Olympiques d'hiver |author=Comité Olympique Suisse |publisher=Imprimerie du Léman |location=Lausanne |year=1928 |language=French |url=http://www.la84foundation.org/6oic/OfficialReports/1928/1928w2.pdf |format=PDF |access-date=1 June 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20101217062055/http://la84foundation.org/6oic/OfficialReports/1928/1928w2.pdf |archive-date=17 December 2010}}</ref>

== Speed skating ==
{{main|Speed skating at the 1928 Winter Olympics}}
[[Speed skating]] competitions were held on 13 and 14 February at the 	Olympia-Eisstadion Badrutts Park in St. Moritz.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/961|title=Speed skating, 500m men|work=[[Olympedia]]|access-date=1 June 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/963|title=Speed skating, 5000m men|work=[[Olympedia]]|access-date=1 June 2024}}</ref> [[Alberts Rumba]] participated in three events in the competition.<ref name="Result"/><ref name="SR"/> This was Rumba's second appearance at the Winter Olympics after his debut in the [[Speed skating at the 1924 Winter Olympics|1924 Games]]. In between the Games, he appeared in two World Championships placing fifth (1926) and seventh (1927). He was the reigning Latvian all-round champion since 1926.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympics.com/en/athletes/alberts-rumba|title=Alberts Rumba|work=[[Olympics.com]]|access-date=1 June 2024}}</ref> In the Games, he recorded a best place finish of 14th in the 1500 m event.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/962|title=Speed skating, 1500m men|work=[[Olympedia]]|access-date=1 June 2024}}</ref>
