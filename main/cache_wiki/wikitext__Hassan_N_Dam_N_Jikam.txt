'''Hassan N'Dam N'Jikam''' (born 18 February 1984) is a Cameroonian-French [[professional boxer]]. He has held [[World Boxing Organization|WBO]] middleweight title in 2012 and the [[WBA (Regular)]] title in 2017, and has challenged once for a [[super-middleweight]] world title in 2019.

==Amateur career==
N'dam N'Jikam won a silver medal in the [[middleweight]] division at the [[boxing at the 2003 All-Africa Games|2003 All-Africa Games]], losing to [[Ramadan Yasser]] of Egypt in the final. N'jikam represented Cameroon in the [[boxing at the 2004 Summer Olympics – Middleweight|middleweight bracket]] at the [[boxing at the 2004 Summer Olympics|2004 Olympics]], defeating [[Juan Ubaldo]] of the Dominican Republic and [[Andy Lee (boxer)|Andy Lee]] of Ireland on close countback decisions, but losing in the quarter-finals to eventual gold medallist [[Gaydarbek Gaydarbekov]] of Russia.<ref>{{Cite web |title=ATHENS 2004 - BOXING 69 - 75 KG MEN RESULTS |url=https://olympics.com/en/olympic-games/athens-2004/results/boxing/69-75-kg-men |access-date=21 September 2023 |website=olympics.com}}</ref>

Following the Olympics, and having no means of boxing professionally in his native Cameroon,  N'Jikam moved to France due to the shared language.<ref>Fischer, Doug (15 February 2014). [http://www.ringtv.com/319193-qa-hassan-ndam-njikam/ "Q&A: Hassan N'dam N'Jikam"]. ''[[The Ring (magazine)|The Ring]]''. Retrieved 29 December 2016.</ref>

At the [[2016 Olympics]], professional boxers were permitted to compete at the Olympics for the first ever time. As one of the few professionals who entered, N'Jikam once again represented Cameroon and qualified for the [[boxing at the 2016 Summer Olympics – Men's light heavyweight|light-heavyweight bracket]], but lost in the first round to [[Michel Borges]] of Brazil. This was seen as a surprise to many boxing observers, who predicted that amateurs would not fare well against experienced professionals.<ref>[[Associated Press]] (7 August 2016). [https://www.usatoday.com/story/sports/olympics/2016/08/06/pro-trip-hassan-ndam-loses-opening-fight-at-rio-olympics/88350836/ "Pro trip: Hassan N'Dam loses opening fight at Rio Olympics"]. Retrieved 28 December 2016.</ref>

==Professional career==
N'Jikam made his professional debut on 4 December 2004, scoring a third-round [[technical knockout|stoppage]] against Alexis Culit. On 30 October 2010, having fought mainly in his adoptive France, N'Jikam won the vacant [[World Boxing Association|WBA]] [[interim championship|interim]] [[middleweight]] title with a close [[unanimous decision]] (117-111, 115-114, 115-114) over [[Avtandil Khurtsidze]] at the [[Palais des sports Marcel-Cerdan]].<ref>LaBate, Chris (30 October 2010). [http://www.boxingscene.com/njikam-decisions-khurtsidze-wins-wba-interim-title--32356 "N'Jikam Decisions Khurtsidze, Wins WBA Interim Title"]. BoxingScene. Retrieved 28 December 2016.</ref>

N'Jikam's only defense of his title was made on 2 April 2011 against [[Giovanni Lorenzo (boxer)|Giovanni Lorenzo]]. He won a wide unanimous decision (119-109, 119-110, 116-111) However, major controversy arose during the fight when N'Jikam was knocked out of the ring by a series of punches from Lorenzo in round five. A WBA official then helped N'Jikam back into the ring, which was against the sanctioning body's rules. Lorenzo's team later argued that this should have been grounds for immediate [[disqualification (boxing)|disqualification]], as well as disputing the overly wide scorecards.<ref>[http://www.boxingscene.com/lorenzo-protests-decision-loss-hassan-ndam-njikam--38151 "Lorenzo Protests Decision Loss To Hassan N'Dam N'Jikam"]. BoxingScene. 15 April 2011. Retrieved 28 December 2016.</ref>

A [[purse bid]] was ordered between N'Jikam and [[WBA (Regular)]] champion [[Gennady Golovkin]]. The purse bid was scheduled for 2 February 2012.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://theboxingtribune.com/2012/01/26/gennady-golovkin-hassan-ndam-njikam-ordered-by-wba-purse-bid-on-february-2/|title=Gennady Golovkin — Hassan N'Dam N'Jikam Ordered by WBA, Purse Bid on February 2|work=The Boxing Tribune |date=26 January 2012}}</ref> However, days before the purse bid  N'Jikam vacated his title rather than face Golovkin.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.boxingscene.com/golovkins-manager-njikam-refused-fight-gennady--49185|title=Golovkin's Manager: N'Jikam Has Refused to Fight Gennady|website=BoxingScene.com}}</ref>

On 4 May 2012, N'Jikam won the vacant [[World Boxing Organization|WBO]] interim middleweight title via unanimous decision against [[Max Bursak]].<ref>Donovan, Jake (4 May 2012). [http://www.boxingscene.com/hassan-ndam-njikam-tops-max-bursak-wins-interim-title--52522 "Hassan N'Dam N'Jikam Tops Max Bursak, Wins Interim Title"] BoxingScene. Retrieved 28 December 2016.</ref>

He was later promoted to full world champion status on 25 August, after [[Dmitry Pirog]] was stripped of the title for choosing to fight [[Gennady Golovkin]] instead of [[mandatory challenger]] N'Jikam.<ref>Stangrit, Gregory (3 September 2012). [http://www.boxingscene.com/dmitry-pirog-not-surprised-wbo-took-away-his-title--56666 "Dmitry Pirog Not Surprised WBO Took Away His Title"]. BoxingScene. Retrieved 28 December 2016.</ref> In his first world title defence on 20 October, as well as his first fight in the United States, N'Jikam suffered six [[knockdown (boxing)|knockdowns]] en route to a unanimous decision loss (107-115, 107-115, 107-115) to [[Peter Quillin]].<ref>[http://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/8183434/peter-quillin-beat-hassan-ndam-to-become-wbo-middleweight-champion "Peter Quillin beat Hassan N'Dam to become WBO middleweight champion"]. ''[[Sky Sports]]''. [[Sky (United Kingdom)|Sky]]. 12 October 2013. Retrieved 28 December 2016.</ref>

N'Jikam won an [[International Boxing Federation|IBF]] title eliminator over [[Curtis Stevens]] with a unanimous decision (119-108, 116-111, 116-111). Stevens was knocked down in round 8.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/11626550/hassan-ndam-wins-unanimous-decision-curtis-stevens|title=N'Dam captures decision win over Stevens|date=2 October 2014|website=ESPN.com}}</ref> A second opportunity to become world champion came on 20 June 2015, against [[David Lemieux (boxer)|David Lemieux]] for the vacant [[International Boxing Federation|IBF]] middleweight title.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2015-06-21|title=Montreal's David Lemieux defeats N'Dam to win IBF middleweight title|url=https://montreal.ctvnews.ca/montreal-s-david-lemieux-defeats-n-dam-to-win-ibf-middleweight-title-1.2433055|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150805180344/http://montreal.ctvnews.ca/montreal-s-david-lemieux-defeats-n-dam-to-win-ibf-middleweight-title-1.2433055|url-status=dead|archive-date=5 August 2015|access-date=2021-01-22|website=Montreal|language=en}}</ref> N'Jikam again endured multiple knockdowns—four in total—but managed to go the distance to lose on the cards (109-115, 109-115, 110-114).<ref>Fischer, Doug (21 June 2015). [http://www.ringtv.com/392513-david-lemieux-outslugs-hassan-ndam-to-earn-ibf-middleweight-title/ "David Lemieux outslugs Hassan N'Dam to earn IBF middleweight title"]. ''[[The Ring (magazine)|The Ring]]''. Retrieved 28 December 2016.</ref>

2016 saw N'Jikam reel off four consecutive victories, with a brief pause in August to compete in the 2016 Olympics. On 17 December, he emphatically re-entered the world title scene by becoming WBA interim middleweight champion for a second time, following a brutal one-punch [[knockout]] of [[Alfonso Blanco (boxer)|Alfonso Blanco]] in only 22 seconds of the first round.<ref>Ecksel, Robert (17 December 2016). [http://www.wbaboxing.com/boxing-news/hassan-ndam-kos-blanco-in-22-seconds "Hassan N'Dam KOs Blanco in 22 Seconds"]. [[World Boxing Association|WBA]]. Retrieved 28 December 2016.</ref>

N'Jikam then won the full ([[WBA (Regular)|Regular]]) title with a controversial [[split decision]] (116-111, 115-112, 110-117) win over [[Ryōta Murata]] on 27 May 2017. N'Jikam was knocked down in round 4 but survived and got the decision. When the result was announced, the crowd at [[Ariake Colosseum]] jeered.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.espn.co.uk/boxing/story/_/id/19426568/hassan-ndam-defeats-ryota-murata-split-decision|title=N'Dam keeps title with split decision vs. Murata|date=20 May 2017|website=ESPN.com}}</ref> The two judges who scored the fight for N'Jikam were immediately suspended and WBA president Gilberto Mendoza issued a public apology.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/19470851/judges-hassan-ndam-ryota-murata-fight-suspended-questionable-scorecards|title=N'Dam-Murata judges suspended over scoring|date=26 May 2017|website=ESPN.com}}</ref> A rematch was immediately ordered by the WBA,<ref>[http://www.eurosport.com/boxing/boxing-wba-order-rematch-of-n-dam-murata-title-fight_sto6174535/story.shtml "Boxing-WBA order rematch of N'Dam-Murata title fight"]. [[Eurosport]]. 21 May 2017. Retrieved 21 May 2017.</ref> and scheduled for 22 October.<ref>[http://www.boxingscene.com/ryota-murata-vs-hassan-ndam-rematch-on-october-22--119133 "Ryota Murata vs. Hassan N'Dam Rematch on October 22"]. 3 August 2017. Retrieved 3 August 2017.</ref>

On 22 October, Murata decisively beat N'Jikam, who threw in the towel after round 7. Murata's body attack wore his opponent down, he was also able to hurt him with a series of combinations. The fight was attended by 8,500 people at the [[Ryōgoku Kokugikan]]. This was N'Jikam's first stoppage loss.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.badlefthook.com/2017/10/22/16518520/ryota-murata-stops-hassan-ndam-in-rematch|title=Murata stops N'Dam in rematch|first=Patrick L.|last=Stumberg|date=22 October 2017|website=Bad Left Hook}}</ref>

On 22 December 2018, N'Jikam managed to overcome a knockdown and beat former title-challenger Martin Murray via a majority-decision win. N'Jikam won on the scorecards, 117-112 and 116-112, while the third judge scored the fight a draw, 114-114.<ref>{{Cite web|last=|first=|date=22 December 2018|title=Hassan N'Dam Overcomes Knockdown, Beats Martin Murray|url=https://www.boxingscene.com/hassan-ndam-beats-martin-murray-results--134864|access-date=2020-07-09|website=BoxingScene.com|language=en-us}}</ref>

On the Joshua vs Ruiz undercard, N'Jikam fought Callum Smith for his WBA world title. Smith would end up dropping N'Jikam three times. Despite getting up from the third knockdown too, the referee decided to stop the fight and award Smith the victory.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Christ|first=Scott|date=2019-06-01|title=Callum Smith blows out Hassan N'Dam in three rounds|url=https://www.badlefthook.com/2019/6/1/18649022/joshua-vs-ruiz-results-callum-smith-knockout-hassan-ndam-tko-dazn-boxing-sky-box-office-canelo-next|access-date=2020-07-09|website=Bad Left Hook|language=en}}</ref>

In his next fight, N'Jikam lost again, this time against WBA #1, WBC #6, IBF #8 and WBC #11 [[Fedor Chudinov]] in Russia.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Chudinov vs N'Dam - News, Tape, Ringwalk, TV, Streaming & Tickets|url=https://box.live/fights/chudinov-vs-jikam/|access-date=2020-07-09|website=Box.Live|language=en}}</ref>

On June 8, 2020, N'Jikam announced that he will transition into MMA, signing with French MMA promotion ARES FC.<ref>{{Cite web|last=|first=|date=8 June 2020|title=Hassan N'Dam Transitions Into MMA, Signs With League ARES FC|url=https://www.boxingscene.com/hassan-ndam-transitions-into-mma-signs-with-league-ares-fc--149381|access-date=2020-07-09|website=BoxingScene.com|language=en-us}}</ref>
