This is a listing of the '''history of the World Record''' in the 100 [[breaststroke]] [[Swimming (sport)|swimming]] event.

The first world record in long course (50&nbsp;metres) [[swimming (sport)|swimming]] was recognized by the [[International Swimming Federation]] (FINA) in [[1961 in sports|1961]], while the women's world record times were officially acknowledged in [[1958 in sports|1958]].{{Citation needed|date=March 2012}} In the short course (25&nbsp;metres) swimming events the world's governing body has recognized world records since 3 March 1991.
