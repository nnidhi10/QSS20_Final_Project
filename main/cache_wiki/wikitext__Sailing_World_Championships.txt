The '''Sailing World Championships''' (formerly '''ISAF Sailing World Championships''') are World championships in sailing for the 10 events contested at the [[Sailing at the Summer Olympics|Summer Olympics]], organized by [[World Sailing]] (formerly ISAF) and held every four years since 2003. In contrast to other years, when each class organise their [[World championships in sailing|World championship]] under supervision of World Sailing, they are the combined World championships for all the disciplines of the next Summer Olympics and serves as the major qualification event for it.

==History==
In 1998, the ISAF World Sailing Championships were held in Dubai. 
The [[2003 ISAF Sailing World Championships|ISAF Sailing World Championships]] was held in [[Cádiz]] in 2003, followed by [[Cascais]] in 2007 and [[Perth]] in 2011.<ref name="sailing.org">{{cite web|url=http://www.sailing.org/events/isafworlds/background.php|title=Background|website=sailing.org}}</ref>

In 2014, [[Santander, Spain|Santander]] hosted the [[2014 ISAF Sailing World Championships|4th ISAF Sailing World Championships]] attracting more than 1,100 competitors, which distributed half of [[Sailing at the 2016 Summer Olympics – Qualification|qualifications]] for the [[Sailing at the 2016 Summer Olympics|2016 Summer Olympics sailing event]].<ref name="sailing.org"/>

The 2018 edition, in [[Aarhus]], Denmark, saw the introduction of [[Formula Kite]] classes.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.sailing.org/2018/08/05/day-4-morning-report-aarhus-sailing-world-championships-2018/ | work=World Sailing | title=Day #4 Morning Report - Aarhus Sailing World Championships 2018 }}</ref> The 2023 event, orginially scheduled for 2002, took place in [[The Hague]], the Netherlands.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.sail-world.com/news/232005/The-Hague-to-become-sailing-capital-in-2023 | title=The Hague to become sailing capital in 2023 }}</ref> The 2023 edition saw the introduction of para classes.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.sail-world.com/news/265633/Allianz-Sailing-Worlds-hailed-as-inspirational | title=2023 Allianz Sailing World Championships in the Hague hailed as inspirational and historic }}</ref>

For the edition before the [[2028 Summer Olympics]], [[World Sailing]] decided to split the event into one championship for one-person events and one for two-person events, with the 2026 event awarded to [[Valencia]], Spain, and the 2027 event awarded to [[Gdynia]], Poland.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.sailing.org/2024/06/06/world-sailing-announces-hosts-of-the-new-format-2026-2027-world-sailing-championships/ | work=World Sailing | title=World Sailing announces hosts of the new-format 2026-2027 World Sailing Championships }}</ref>
