[[Mongolia]] sent a delegation to compete at the [[1994 Winter Olympics]] in [[Lillehammer]], Norway from 12 to 27 February 1994.  The Mongolian delegation consisted of a single [[short track speed skater]] [[Batchuluuny Bat-Orgil]].  He competed in two events, where he finished the [[Short track speed skating at the 1994 Winter Olympics – Men's 500 metres|500 metres]] event in 24th place and the [[Short track speed skating at the 1994 Winter Olympics – Men's 1000 metres|1000 metres]] competition in 29th position.

==Background==
The [[Mongolian National Olympic Committee]] was recognized by the [[International Olympic Committee]] on 1 January 1962,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympic.org/mongolia|title=Mongolia – National Olympic Committee (NOC)|publisher=[[International Olympic Committee]]|access-date=15 May 2018|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180123214742/https://www.olympic.org/mongolia|archive-date=23 January 2018}}</ref> and the nation entered Olympic competition soon after, talking part in both the [[1964 Winter Olympics|1964 Winter]] and [[1964 Summer Olympics|Summer Olympics]]. Mongolia has only missed two Olympic Games since, the [[1976 Winter Olympics]]; and the [[1984 Summer Olympics]] as the Mongolians joined in the [[1984 Olympics boycott|Soviet-led boycott]] of the Games in [[Los Angeles]].<ref name="MONSR">{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/countries/MGL/|title=Mongolia|publisher=Sports Reference|access-date=15 May 2018|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180310140036/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/countries/MGL/|archive-date=10 March 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.nytimes.com/1984/05/15/world/olympics-decision-final-soviet-says.html|title=Olympics Decision Final, Soviet Says|last=Schmemann|first=Serge|date=15 May 1984|work=[[The New York Times]]|page=A1|access-date=15 May 2018|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180331100154/https://www.nytimes.com/1984/05/15/world/olympics-decision-final-soviet-says.html|archive-date=31 March 2018}}</ref>  Lillehammer was Mongolia's seventh appearance at a Winter Olympics.<ref name="MONSR" />  The 1994 Winter Olympics were held from 12 to 27 February 1994; a total of 1,737 athletes representing 67 [[National Olympic Committees]] took part.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympic.org/lillehammer-1994|title=Lillehammer 1994 Winter Olympics – results & video highlights|publisher=[[International Olympic Committee]]|access-date=11 August 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180626172324/https://www.olympic.org/lillehammer-1994|archive-date=26 June 2018|url-status=live|df=dmy-all}}</ref> [[Batchuluuny Bat-Orgil]], a short-track speed skater, was the only competitor Mongolia sent to Lillehammer.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/countries/MGL/winter/1994/|title=Mongolia at the 1994 Lillehammer Winter Games|publisher=Sports Reference|access-date=19 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170822015749/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/countries/MGL/winter/1994/|archive-date=2017-08-22|url-status=dead}}</ref>  He was selected as the flag-bearer for the [[1994 Winter Olympics opening ceremony|opening ceremony]].<ref name="MONSR" />

==Short track speed skating==
{{main|Short track speed skating at the 1994 Winter Olympics}}

[[Batchuluuny Bat-Orgil]] was 24 years old at the time of the Lillehammer Olympics, and was making his only Olympic appearance.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympic.org/bat-orgil-batchuluun|title=Bat-Orgil BATCHULUUN – Olympic Short Track Speed Skating – Mongolia|publisher=[[International Olympic Committee]]|access-date=19 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161006151503/https://www.olympic.org/bat-orgil-batchuluun|archive-date=2016-10-06|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="SRBat">{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/ba/batchuluuny-bat-orgil-1.html|title=Batchuluuny Bat-Orgil Bio, Stats, and Results|publisher=Sports Reference|access-date=19 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170630195149/http://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/ba/batchuluuny-bat-orgil-1.html|archive-date=2017-06-30|url-status=dead}}</ref>  He entered two events: the [[Short track speed skating at the 1994 Winter Olympics – Men's 500 metres|500 metres]] and the [[Short track speed skating at the 1994 Winter Olympics – Men's 1000 metres|1000 metres]].<ref name="SRBat" />  The 1000 metres race was held on 22 February, and he was drawn into heat one, which he finished in a time of 1 minute and 40.41 seconds, which was fourth and last in his heat.  Only the top two from each heat were allowed to advance, and as a result Batchuluuny was eliminated.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/winter/1994/STK/mens-1000-metres-round-one.html|title=Short Track Speed Skating at the 1994 Lillehammer Winter Games: Men's 1,000 metres Round One|publisher=Sports Reference|access-date=19 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170707100854/http://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/winter/1994/STK/mens-1000-metres-round-one.html|archive-date=2017-07-07|url-status=dead}}</ref>    The gold medal was won by [[Kim Ki-hoon]] of [[South Korea at the 1994 Winter Olympics|South Korea]], the silver was won by his fellow South Korean [[Chae Ji-hoon]], and the bronze by [[Marc Gagnon]] of [[Canada at the 1994 Winter Olympics|Canada]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympic.org/lillehammer-1994/short-track-speed-skating/1000m-men|title=Lillehammer 1994 1000m men – Olympic Short Track Speed Skating|publisher=[[International Olympic Committee]]|access-date=19 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170326123121/https://www.olympic.org/lillehammer-1994/short-track-speed-skating/1000m-men|archive-date=2017-03-26|url-status=live}}</ref>

The 500 metres race was held from 24 to 26 February,<ref name="500SR">{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/winter/1994/STK/mens-500-metres.html|title=Short Track Speed Skating at the 1994 Lillehammer Winter Games: Men's 500 metres|publisher=Sports Reference|access-date=19 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180914203449/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/winter/1994/STK/mens-500-metres.html|archive-date=2018-09-14|url-status=dead}}</ref> and Batchuluuny was drawn into heat four.  He finished the heat in 48.63 seconds, third out of three finishers in his heat, but only the top two in each heat were allowed to advance, and he was eliminated.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/winter/1994/STK/mens-500-metres-round-one.html|title=Short Track Speed Skating at the 1994 Lillehammer Winter Games: Men's 500 metres Round One|publisher=Sports Reference|access-date=19 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170707101139/http://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/winter/1994/STK/mens-500-metres-round-one.html|archive-date=2017-07-07|url-status=dead}}</ref>  Based on his heat time, he was classified as 24th place out of 31 competitors.<ref name="500SR" />  The gold medal was eventually won by Chae Ji-hoon of South Korea, the silver was taken by [[Mirko Vuillermin]] of [[Italy at the 1994 Winter Olympics|Italy]], and the bronze medal was won by [[Nicky Gooch]] of [[Great Britain at the 1994 Winter Olympics|Great Britain]].<ref name="IOCSR1994">{{cite web|url=https://www.olympic.org/lillehammer-1994/short-track-speed-skating|title=Lillehammer 1994 Short Track Speed Skating – Results & Videos|publisher=[[International Olympic Committee]]|access-date=19 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180323215638/https://www.olympic.org/lillehammer-1994/short-track-speed-skating|archive-date=2018-03-23|url-status=live}}</ref>
