'''Sanita Pušpure''' ({{IPA|lv|ˈsɑn̪it̪ɑ ˈpuʃpure|}}; born 21 December 1981) is a Latvian-born Irish [[Rowing (sport)|rower]]. She was a back-to-back world champion in the women's single scull, winning her title at the [[2018 World Rowing Championships]] in Plovdiv<ref name="worldgold">{{cite news|title=Dominant Sanita Puspure powers to world rowing gold for Ireland|url=https://www.rte.ie/sport/rowing/2018/0916/994069-sanita-puspure/|accessdate=17 September 2018|newspaper=RTÉ|date=16 September 2018}}</ref> and defending it at the [[2019 World Rowing Championships]] in Ottensheim. She initially competed for Latvia at a junior level, but she moved to Ireland in 2006 and began competing for her adopted country in 2010 before gaining full Irish nationality in 2011.<ref name=introducing/><ref name=scrambles>{{cite news|title=Puspure scrambles onto Irish team|url=http://www.irishtimes.com/sports/other/2012/0523/1224316601643.html|accessdate=17 July 2012|newspaper=Irish Times|date=23 May 2012}}</ref> She was selected as the sole rowing competitor for Ireland at the [[2012 Summer Olympics]]. In May 2016, she qualified for the [[Rowing at the 2016 Summer Olympics – Women's single sculls|Women's single sculls]] at the [[2016 Summer Olympics]].<ref>{{cite news | url = http://www.irishtimes.com/sport/other-sports/sanita-puspure-the-latest-to-reach-rio-1.2659229 | title = Sanita Puspure the latest to reach Rio | first = Liam | last = Gorman | newspaper = Irish Times | date = 24 May 2016| accessdate = 5 August 2016}}</ref><ref name="worldgold"/> Pušpure is now head coach of UCC Rowing Club in [[Cork (city)|Cork]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Quinn |first=Gavin |date=2021-11-18 |title=Former world champion Sanita Puspure appointed head coach of UCC |url=https://www.irishmirror.ie/sport/other-sport/former-world-champion-sanita-puspure-25493034 |access-date=2022-11-13 |website=Irish Mirror |language=en}}</ref>

==Career==
She began her rowing career in her native Latvia. In 2003, she placed third in the single scull competition at the World under-23 Championships. In the following year took the gold medal in the double scull at the World Student Games.<ref name=rtesanita/>

She moved to Ireland in 2006, when her husband Kaspar got a job at [[Dublin Airport]],<ref>{{cite news|title=Sanita's Olympic bid – a special appeal for your assistance|url=http://oldcollegians.ie/2012/06/sanitas-olympic-bid-a-special-appeal/|accessdate=17 July 2012|newspaper=Old Collegians Boat Club|date=6 June 2012}}</ref><ref name=introducing>{{cite news|title=London 2012: Introducing… Sanita Puspure |url=http://www.thescore.ie/london-2012-introducing-sanita-puspure-507139-Jul2012/ |accessdate=17 July 2012 |newspaper=The Score |date=2 July 2012 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20120722064846/http://www.thescore.ie/london-2012-introducing-sanita-puspure-507139-Jul2012/ |archivedate=22 July 2012 }}</ref> and they initially lived in [[Cork (city)|Cork]].<ref name=scrambles/> In 2009, she won the single sculls event at the Irish Championships,<ref name=rtesanita /> repeating that success at the following year's competition and taking the double sculls title too.<ref name=oldcoll/>

Pušpure began competing for Ireland in World Cup events during 2010 but was only allowed to compete at the World Championships after she gained Irish nationality in 2011. She was in the winning teams in the double and quad sculls at the [[Henley Women's Regatta]] in 2011.<ref name=oldcoll/> She initially aimed to qualify for the 2012 Olympics in the double scull, partnered with [[Lisa Dilleen]]. The duo finished in twelfth place at the [[2011 World Rowing Championships|2011 World Championships]], where the top eight teams qualified for the Olympics.<ref name=sealsplace/>

She competed in the single scull event for the first time at a World Cup event in [[Belgrade]] in May 2012, finishing in fifth place.<ref>{{cite news|title=Rowing Round–up: Puspure Fifth in Belgrade, 2012}}</ref> After finishing third in the semi,<ref>{{cite news|title=Puspure in Final at Olympic Qualification rowing Regatta|url=http://afloat.ie/watersport/rowing/rowing-news/item/18796-puspure-in-final-at-olympic-qualification-rowing-regatta|accessdate=17 July 2012|newspaper=Afloat|date=22 May 2012}}</ref> she placed fourth in the final, giving Ireland a place in the women's single sculls competition at the [[2012 Summer Olympics]].<ref>{{cite news|title=Ireland Olympian Sanita Puspure is Afloat Rower of [[Ireland at the 2012 Summer Olympics|Irish team]] at the Games as the country's only rowing competitor, and their first female single sculler since the [[1980 Summer Olympics]].<ref In September 2018 she won the single sculls at the [[2018 World Rowing Championships|world championships]] in [[Plovdiv]], Bulgaria, her first world title.<ref name="worldgold"/> She defended her title a year later in Linz-Ottensheim, Austria, and also qualified at the event for the 2020 Olympics.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.irishtimes.com/sport/other-sports/sanita-puspure-defends-her-world-championship-gold-in-austria-1.4004063|title=Sanita Puspure defends her World Championship gold in Austria|date=1 September 2019|newspaper=Irish Times}}</ref> Puspure was selected as a 2021 [[2020 Summer Olympics|Tokyo Olympian]] but had to settle for the B final in the women's single scull.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rte.ie/sport/olympics/2021/0729/1237889-tokyo-2020-disappointment-for-sanita-puspure/|title=Tokyo 2020: Disappointment for Sanita Puspure, who has to settle for B final|date=5 August 2021|publisher=RTE Sport}}</ref>

==Personal life==
She lives with her family in [[Ballincollig]], [[County Cork]].<ref name=sealsplace>{{cite news|last=Gorman|first=Liam|title=Puspure seals place at Games|url=http://www.irishtimes.com/newspaper/sport/2012/0524/1224316608174.html|accessdate=17 July 2012|newspaper=Irish Times|date=24 May 2012}}</ref> Both of her children were born in Ireland.<ref name=oldcoll>{{cite news|last=Kelly|first=James|title=Sanita Puspure – competing in Olympic qualifiers|url=http://oldcollegians.ie/2012/05/sanita-puspure-competing-in-olympic-qualifiers/|accessdate=17 July 2012|newspaper=Old Collegians Boat Club|date=15 May 2012|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304002326/http://oldcollegians.ie/2012/05/sanita-puspure-competing-in-olympic-qualifiers/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Pušpure races for Old Collegians Boat Club.<ref>{{cite news|title=Puspure joins Ireland's to compete for Ireland|url=http://www.irishexaminer.com/breakingnews/sport/puspure-joins-irelands-to-compete-for-ireland-552640.html|accessdate=17 July 2012|newspaper=Irish Examiner|date=23 May 2012}}</ref>
