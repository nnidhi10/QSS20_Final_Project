'''Valery Kimovich Shomin ''' (born 31 July 1971, in [[Moscow]], [[Soviet Union]])<ref name=sref>{{cite web|title=Valeriy Shomin Biography|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/sh/valery-shomin-1.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200417214705/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/sh/valery-shomin-1.html|url-status=dead|archive-date=17 April 2020|access-date=5 June 2011}}</ref> is a [[Sport shooting|sport shooter]] who has thrice represented [[Russia at the Olympics|Russia at the Summer Olympics]].

At the [[2004 Summer Olympics]] in [[Athens]] he participated in the [[Shooting at the 2004 Summer Olympics – Men's skeet|men's skeet]] event, finishing tied for 15th position.<ref name=sref/> At the [[2008 Summer Olympics|2008 Games]] in [[Beijing]] he finished 19th in the [[Shooting at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's skeet|men's skeet]].<ref name=sref/>  At the [[2012 Summer Olympics|2012 Games]] he finished 4th in the [[Shooting at the 2012 Summer Olympics – Men's skeet|men's skeet]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympic.org/london-2012/shooting/skeet-125-targets-men|title=London 2012 skeet 125 targets men - Olympic Shooting|date=26 January 2017|website=International Olympic Committee}}</ref>
