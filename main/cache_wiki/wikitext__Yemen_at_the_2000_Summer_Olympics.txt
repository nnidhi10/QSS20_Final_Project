[[Yemen]] sent a delegation to compete at the [[2000 Summer Olympics]] in [[Sydney]], Australia from 15 September to 1 October 2000.  This was their third appearance at a [[Summer Olympic Games]] as a unified country.  The Yemeni delegation consisted of two [[track and field]] athletes, [[Basheer Al-Khewani]] and [[Hana Ali Saleh]].  Neither advanced beyond the first round of their respective events.

==Athletics==
{{main article|Athletics at the 2000 Summer Olympics}}

Basheer Al-Khewani was 18 years old at the time of the Sydney Olympics, and was making his only Olympic appearance.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/al/basheer-al-khewani-1.html|title=Basheer Al-Khewani Bio, Stats, and Results|publisher=Sports Reference|access-date=24 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181025031321/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/al/basheer-al-khewani-1.html|archive-date=25 October 2018|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympic.org/basheer-al-khewani|title=Basheer AL KHEWANI – Olympic Athletics – Yemen|publisher=[[International Olympic Committee]]|access-date=24 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181025070928/https://www.olympic.org/basheer-al-khewani|archive-date=25 October 2018|url-status=live}}</ref> On 22 September he took part in the first round of the [[Athletics at the 2000 Summer Olympics – Men's 400 metres|men's 400 meters]], and was drawn into heat eight.  He finished his race in a time of 49.72 seconds, eighth and last in his heat, and was therefore eliminated.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/summer/2000/ATH/mens-400-metres-round-one.html|title=Athletics at the 2000 Sydney Summer Games: Men's 400 metres Round One|publisher=Sports Reference|access-date=24 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181025070914/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/summer/2000/ATH/mens-400-metres-round-one.html|archive-date=25 October 2018|url-status=dead}}</ref> The gold medal was won by [[Michael Johnson (sprinter)|Michael Johnson]] of the [[United States at the 2000 Summer Olympics|United States]] in 43.84 seconds, the silver was taken by fellow American [[Alvin Harrison]], and the bronze medal was earned by [[Greg Haughton]] of [[Jamaica at the 2000 Summer Olympics|Jamaica]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/summer/2000/ATH/mens-400-metres.html|title=Athletics at the 2000 Sydney Summer Games: Men's 400 metres|publisher=Sports Reference|access-date=24 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181025031318/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/summer/2000/ATH/mens-400-metres.html|archive-date=25 October 2018|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympic.org/sydney-2000/athletics/400m-men|title=Sydney 2000 400m men – Olympic Sydney 2000 Athletics|publisher=[[International Olympic Committee]]|access-date=24 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170327060208/https://www.olympic.org/sydney-2000/athletics/400m-men|archive-date=27 March 2017|url-status=live}}</ref>

Hana Ali Saleh was 32 years old at the time, and also making her only appearance at an Olympic Games.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/sa/hana-ali-saleh-1.html|title=Hana Ali Saleh Bio, Stats, and Results|publisher=Sports Reference|access-date=24 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181025071012/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/sa/hana-ali-saleh-1.html|archive-date=25 October 2018|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympic.org/hana-ali-saleh|title=Hana Ali SALEH – Olympic Athletics – Yemen|publisher=[[International Olympic Committee]]|access-date=24 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181025031325/https://www.olympic.org/hana-ali-saleh|archive-date=25 October 2018|url-status=live}}</ref> She was the first woman to represent Yemen at the Olympics.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://nationalyemen.com/2012/07/22/yemeni-athletes-travel-to-london-for-2012-olympic-games/|title=Yemeni athletes travel to London for 2012 Olympic Games|publisher=National Yemen Newspaper|access-date=24 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181025070920/https://nationalyemen.com/2012/07/22/yemeni-athletes-travel-to-london-for-2012-olympic-games/|archive-date=25 October 2018|url-status=live}}</ref> On 27 September she took part in the first round of the [[Athletics at the 2000 Summer Olympics – Women's 200 metres|women's 200 meters]], and was drawn into heat two.  She finished the race in 30.36 seconds, eighth and last in her heat. Her time was over six seconds behind seventh place; therefore she was eliminated at this stage.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/summer/2000/ATH/womens-200-metres-round-one.html|title=Athletics at the 2000 Sydney Summer Games: Women's 200 metres Round One|publisher=Sports
