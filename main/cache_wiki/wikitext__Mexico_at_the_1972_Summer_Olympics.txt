[[Mexico]], the previous host of the [[1968 Summer Olympics]] in [[Mexico City]], competed at the [[1972 Summer Olympics]] in [[Munich]], [[West Germany]]. 174 competitors, 152 men and 22 women, took part in 112 events in 20 sports.<ref name="sports-reference">{{cite web |url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/countries/MEX/summer/1972/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200417055238/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/countries/MEX/summer/1972/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=17 April 2020 |title=Mexico at the 1972 Munich Summer Games |access-date=13 October 2014 |work=sports-reference.com}}</ref>

==Medalists==

===Silver===
* [[Alfonso Zamora]] &mdash; Boxing, Men's Bantamweight

==Archery==
{{main article|Archery at the 1972 Summer Olympics}}

In the first modern archery competition at the Olympics, Mexico entered three men and three women. The women were much more successful than the men, though none of the archers won a medal. Their highest placing competitor was [[Francisca de Gutierrez]], at 10th place in the women's competition.

'''Women's Individual Competition:'''
* [[Francisca de Gutierrez]] &ndash; 2353 points (→ 10th place)
* [[Aurora Bretón]] &ndash; 2335 points (→ 15th place)
* [[Silvia de Tapia]] &ndash; 2258 points (→ 23rd place)

'''Men's Individual Competition:'''
* [[Alfonso Jones]] &ndash; 2344 points (→ 28th place)
* [[Rafael Aveleira]] &ndash; 2178 points (→ 51st place)
* [[José Almanzor]] &ndash; 2175 points (→ 52nd place)
