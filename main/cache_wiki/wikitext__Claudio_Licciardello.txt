'''Claudio Licciardello''' (born 11 January 1986 in [[Catania]]) is a [[track and field]] sprinter from [[Italy]], who specializes in the [[400 metres]]. His personal best time is 45.25 seconds, achieved in the heats at the 2008 Olympics in [[Beijing]].<ref name=bio/>

==Biography==
At the [[2009 European Indoor Championships in Athletics|2009 European Indoor Championships]] he won the silver medal in the 400 metres,<ref name=bio>{{World Athletics}}</ref> and a gold medal in the [[4 × 400 metres relay]] together with teammates [[Jacopo Marin]], [[Matteo Galvan]] and [[Domenico Rao]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sportresult.com/sports/la/framework/eaa2.asp?event_id=10000100000083&comp_id=28693&module=competition&show=RL&lang=en#round2360040|title=Results. 4x400m Relay Men|date=8 March 2009|website=sportresult.com|publisher=European Athletics|accessdate=9 March 2009|archive-date=10 April 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090410074639/http://www.sportresult.com/sports/la/framework/eaa2.asp?event_id=10000100000083&comp_id=28693&module=competition&show=RL&lang=en#round2360040|url-status=dead}}</ref> He also competed at the [[2004 World Junior Championships in Athletics|2004 World Junior Championships]], the [[2006 European Athletics Championships|2006 European Championships]] and the [[Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Men's 400 metres|2008 Olympic Games]] without reaching the final.<ref name=bio/>
