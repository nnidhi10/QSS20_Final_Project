'''John Joseph Nevin''' (born 7 June 1989) is an Irish [[professional boxer]]. He is a two-time Olympian, and a [[2012 Summer Olympics|London 2012]] silver medalist.

==Early life==
John Joseph Nevin was born in [[Mullingar]], [[County Westmeath]], and is a member of the [[Irish Traveller|Traveller]] community.<ref>{{cite news|last=O'Connor|first=Brendan|title=Katie KOs Angela Merkel and co|url=http://www.independent.ie/national-news/brendan-oconnor-katie-kos-angela-merkel-and-co-3197107.html|access-date=14 August 2012|newspaper=Irish Independent|date=13 August 2012}}</ref>

==Amateur career==
===2008 | Olympic Games qualifying tournament===
Nevin qualified for the [[2008 Summer Olympics|2008 Beijing Olympics]] by winning the first [[1st AIBA European 2008 Olympic Qualifying Tournament|Olympic Qualifying Tournament]] in [[Italy]].

===2008 | European Union Amateur Championships===
Nevin won the 2008 [[2008 European Union boxing championships|EU Amateur Championships]] in [[Cetniewo]], [[Poland]].

===2008 | Olympic Games===
At just 18 years of age, Nevin competed in [[2008 Summer Olympics|Beijing]] at his first [[Summer Olympic Games|Summer Olympics]]. He beat [[Abdelhalim Ourradi]] 9–4 in his first round before being beaten by [[Enkhbatyn Badar-Uugan|Badar-Uugan Enkhbatyn]] 9–2 in the round of 16. Enkhbatyn went on to win gold in the final.

===2009 | World Amateur Championships===
On 9 September 2009, Nevin guaranteed Ireland a historic sixth medal at the [[2009 World Amateur Boxing Championships]] after he beat China's Yu Gu in Milan. He won his quarter-final 7–5.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.rte.ie/sport/boxing/2009/0909/nevinjj_worldchampionships.html |title=Nevin secures medal on way to semis |date=9 September 2009 |work=RTÉ Sport |access-date=9 September 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090910202323/http://www.rte.ie/sport/boxing/2009/0909/nevinjj_worldchampionships.html |archive-date=10 September 2009 }}</ref>
In the semi-final he lost by 5–4 to Russian Eduard Abzalimov, and so took away a bronze medal.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.rte.ie/sport/boxing/2009/0911/nevinjj.html |title=Nevin must settle for bronze |date=11 September 2009 |work=RTÉ Sport |access-date=12 September 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090914012115/http://www.rte.ie/sport/boxing/2009/0911/nevinjj.html |archive-date=14 September 2009 }}</ref>

===2011 | World Amateur Championships===
At the [[2011 World Amateur Boxing Championships|2011 World Boxing Championships]] in [[Baku]], [[Azerbaijan]], Nevin reached the semi-finals after beating Orzubek Shaymov of Uzbekistan 19–17 in the quarter's. Nevin lost to Britain's Luke Campbell in the semi's through a very controversial countback, forcing him to settle for a second World Championship bronze medal. This however was more than enough to qualify Nevin for the 2012 London Olympics.

===2012 | Olympic Games===
Nevin won his first fight in [[2012 Olympic Games|London]], defeating Denmark's [[Dennis Ceylan]] by a wide margin of 21–6.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.rte.ie/sport/olympics/boxing/2012/0728/330949-day-1-boxing|work=RTÉ News|title=Boxing: John Joe Nevin and Darren O'Neill both recorded comfortable round of 32 boxing wins|date=28 July 2012}}</ref> In his second fight of the games he saw off Kazakhstan's [[Kanat Abutalipov]] by a margin of 15–10.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.irishtimes.com/sports/other/2012/0801/1224321275094.html|newspaper=The Irish Times|access-date=4 May 2017|title=Nevin defeats Abutalipov, 15–10}}</ref> In his quarter-final bout he beat [[Oscar Valdez]] to secure a semi-final place and the guarantee of at least a bronze medal.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.rte.ie/sport/olympics/boxing/2012/0805/332204-boxing-nevin-takes-bronze-after|work=RTÉ News|title=Boxing: John Joe Nevin wins Ireland's first medal at the London Olympics after 19–13 win over Oscar Valdez Fierro|date=5 August 2012}}</ref>

He reached the final after defeating the reigning bantamweight world champion [[Lázaro Álvarez]] of Cuba, 19–14.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.independent.ie/sport/other-sports/olympics-2012/irish-news/john-joe-nevin-gets-to-olympic-final-with-silver-medal-3196361.html|work=Irish Independent|title=Olympic final and silver medal for John Joe Nevin}}</ref> In the final however, Nevin lost 14–11 to Britain's [[Luke Campbell (boxer)|Luke Campbell]], winning a silver medal in the process.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.london2012.com/boxing/event/men-bantam-56kg/index.html |title=London 2012 56kg men - Olympic Boxing |website=London2012.com |access-date=2017-05-04 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130402105957/http://www.london2012.com/boxing/event/men-bantam-56kg/index.html |archive-date=2 April 2013 |df=dmy-all }}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/av/olympics/19227509|title=Luke Campbell wins Great Britain's second gold|date= 11 August 2012|work=BBC Sport|access-date=4 December 2012 }}</ref> Nevin reportedly said that he wants his legacy from the Games to be "a closer relationship between Travellers and the settled community."<ref>{{cite news|last=Hickey|first=Shane|title=Games can bring Travellers and settled people together – Nevin|url=http://www.independent.ie/sport/other-sports/olympics-2012/irish-news/games-can-bring-travellers-and-settled-people-together-nevin-3197418.html|access-date=14 August 2012|newspaper=Irish Independent|date=13 August 2012|author2=Caroline Crawford}}</ref>

===2013: European Amateur Championships===
In June 2013 he won a gold medal at the [[2013 European Amateur Boxing Championships]] in the Bantamweight division.<ref>{{cite web|url= http://www.independent.ie/sport/john-joe-nevin-and-jason-quigley-claim-european-gold-in-minsk-29330468.html|title=John Joe Nevin and Jason Quigley claim European gold in Minsk|date=8 June 2013|work=Irish Independent|access-date=10 June 2013 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.thescore.ie/john-joe-nevin-wins-european-bantamweight-gold-942518-Jun2013/ |title=Nevin and Quigley win European boxing gold for Ireland |date=8 June 2013 |work=The Score |access-date=10 June 2013 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131029192515/http://www.thescore.ie/john-joe-nevin-wins-european-bantamweight-gold-942518-Jun2013/ |archive-date=29 October 2013 }}</ref>

===National Titles===
In 2012, Nevin won his fifth straight national senior title, incidentally beating his cousin Michael Nevin 23-3 in the final. Nevin was unable to defend his national title in 2013 because he injured his wrist while sparring in the high performance, the title was taken by Declan Geraghty from Crumlin boxing club. That didn't stop the Irish Amateur Boxing Association from picking Nevin as their #1 choice for the 2013 European Boxing Championships being held in Minsk later that year.{{citation needed|date=May 2017}}

==Professional career==
Having announced the previous year that he planned on turning professional,<ref>{{cite news |last=Hogan |first=Vincent |author-link=Vincent Hogan |work=Irish Independent |date=6 October 2012 |url=http://www.independent.ie/sport/other-sports/john-joe-stepping-into-a-starker-meaner-world-3251221.html |access-date=6 October 2012|title=John Joe stepping into a starker, meaner world}}</ref> Nevin finally did so in 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.irishexaminer.com/sport/other-sports/nevin-starts-likely-exodus-247225.html|title= Nevin starts likely exodus|date=22 October 2013|work=Irish Examiner|access-date=22 October 2013}}</ref>
Speaking on 22 October, he said "This is the next step for me. I've done everything I wanted to do in the amateur business, if I could change the colour of the Olympic medal I would but that's done and now it's time to move on and hopefully bring back a world title to Ireland." Nevin signed a management deal with a business sports partnership that includes GreenBlood Boxing from Philadelphia and Berkley Sports & Media from London.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.independent.ie/sport/other-sports/olympic-hero-john-joe-nevin-turns-pro-29686696.html|title=Olympic hero John Joe Nevin turns pro|work=Irish Independent|date=22 October 2013 |access-date=22 October 2013}}</ref>

Nevin made his professional debut in a [[super featherweight]] bout on 17 March 2014 against Alberto Candelaria,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.independent.ie/sport/other-sports/nevin-set-for-pro-debut-30045919.html|title=Nevin set for pro debut|website=Independent.ie|date=27 February 2014 |access-date=27 February 2014}}</ref> winning by unanimous decision.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.irishtimes.com/sport/other-sports/john-joe-nevin-wins-on-pro-debut-in-boston-1.1729341 |title=John Joe Nevin wins on pro debut in Boston |website=Irishtimes.com|access-date=18 March 2014}}</ref>

After a five year break<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-07-22 |title=John Joe Nevin Returning to the Ring after five-year absence |url=https://www.westmeathexaminer.ie/2024/07/22/john-joe-nevin-returning-to-the-ring-after-five-year-absence/ |access-date=2025-06-19 |website=Westmeath Examiner |language=en-US}}</ref> from the sport he returned to fighting in August 2024 with a points victory over Jose Hernandez Flores.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nevin wins after very long layoff |url=https://www.irishecho.com/2024/8/nevin-wins-after-very-long-layoff |access-date=2025-06-19 |website=Irish Echo Newspaper |language=en}}</ref> In February 2025 he defeated Gerson Escobar Romero in one round.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Explosive Nevin wins it in 1st |url=https://www.irishecho.com/2025/2/explosive-nevin-wins-it-in-1st |access-date=2025-06-19 |website=Irish Echo Newspaper |language=en}}</ref> Nevin moved his record to 17-0 in April 2025 defeating Jonathan Chavez after two rounds. Nevin moved to 18-0 in November 2025 with a win by unanimous decision over the Mexican boxer, Juan Perez Vazquez.{{Citation needed|date=November 2025|reason=Just waiting for someone to report the result}}

==Personal life==
He is married with three children.

== Controversy ==
Nevin has frequently attracted controversy through his involvement in several high profile public order incidents. In 2013, Nevin was arrested and charged following a drunken altercation with his father in a public street in Mullingar.<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.independent.ie/irish-news/courts/olympic-hero-john-joe-nevin-aggressive-to-gardai-and-father-during-street-row-court-hears-29775279.html|title=Olympic hero John Joe Nevin 'aggressive' to gardai and father during street row, court hears - Independent.ie|work=Independent.ie|access-date=2017-02-28|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.rte.ie/news/2013/1205/491116-john-joe-nevin/?RTEMAILID|title=Nevin to give boxing class after probation ruling|date=2013-12-05|work=RTE.ie|access-date=2017-02-28|language=en}}</ref>
