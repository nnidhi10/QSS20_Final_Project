'''Hana Říčná''' (born 20 December 1968) is a Czech former [[artistic gymnast]]. Competing for [[Czechoslovakia]], she won two medals at the [[World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|World Championships]] – a silver on the [[balance beam]] in [[1983 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1983]] and a bronze on the uneven bars in [[1985 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1985]]. She competed at the [[1984 Friendship Games]] and won a silver medal in the all-around and on the balance beam. She also won a silver medal on beam at the [[1985 European Women's Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1985 European Championships]]. She represented [[Czechoslovakia at the 1988 Summer Olympics]].

== Gymnastics career ==
Říčná began competing at the senior level in 1983 and finished 13th in the all-around at the [[1983 European Women's Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1983 European Championships]]. She qualified for the uneven bars and floor exercise final, finishing eighth and sixth, respectively.<ref>{{cite web |title=1983 European Championships Women's Results |url=https://backend.europeangymnastics.com/sites/default/files/paragraph/age-group-competition-info/competition-results/Results_25.pdf |website=[[European Gymnastics]] |access-date=11 June 2025}}</ref> Then at the [[1983 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1983 World Championships]], she helped the Czechoslovak team finish sixth. She advanced to the all-around final and finished 14th. She also advanced to the uneven bars final and finished eighth. Then in the balance beam final, she won the silver medal behind [[Olga Mostepanova]].<ref>{{cite web |title=1983 World Gymnastics Championships Budapest, Hungary October 23-30, 1983 |url=https://static.usagym.org/PDFs/Results/worlds_artistic_results_1983.pdf |website=[[USA Gymnastics]] |access-date=11 June 2025}}</ref>

Říčná finished seventh in the all-around at the 1984 [[American Cup]] and tied with [[Mary Lou Retton]] for the balance beam title.<ref>{{cite web |title=1984 McDonald's American Cup New York, NY |url=https://static.usagym.org/PDFs/Results/americancup_results_1984.pdf |website=[[USA Gymnastics]] |access-date=11 June 2025}}</ref> Due to Czechoslovakia joining the [[Soviet-led boycott of the 1984 Summer Olympics]], she competed at the [[1984 Friendship Games]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Ten East Bloc Countries Agree to Hold Their Own Summer Games |access-date=11 June 2025 |url=https://www.upi.com/Archives/1984/05/24/Ten-East-Bloc-countries-agreed-Thursday-to-hold-their/9546454219200/ |work=[[United Press International]] |date=24 May 1984 |language=en}}</ref> There, she won the all-around silver medal behind Mostepanova despite scoring [[Perfect 10 (gymnastics)|perfect 10s]] on the vault and uneven bars.<ref>{{cite news |title=Female gymnast gets four 10s |url=https://www.newspapers.com/article/the-central-new-jersey-home-news-female/174327756/ |access-date=11 June 2025 |agency=[[The Central New Jersey Home News]] |publisher=[[Associated Press]] |date=26 August 1984 |page=33 |via=[[Newspapers.com]]}}</ref> She helped her country win the bronze medal in the team event.<ref>{{cite web |title=1984 CSSR Spartakiade (Olomouc) - Women's Team |url=https://www.gymn-forum.net/Results/AltGames/Women/1984_team.html |website=Gymn Forum |access-date=11 June 2025 |date=7 December 1998}}</ref> She also finished second to Mostepanova in the balance beam final. She tied for fourth place in the balance beam final and finished eighth in the uneven bars final.<ref>{{cite web |title=1984 CSSR Spartakiade (Olomouc) Women's EF |url=https://www.gymn-forum.net/Results/AltGames/Women/1984_ef.html |website=Gymn Forum |access-date=11 June 2025 |date=5 December 1998}}</ref>

Říčná finished fourth in the all-around, uneven bars, and floor exercise finals at the [[1985 European Women's Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1985 European Championships]]. She did win the silver medal on the balance beam behind [[Oksana Omelianchik]].<ref>{{cite web |title=1985 European Championships Women's Results |url=https://backend.europeangymnastics.com/sites/default/files/paragraph/age-group-competition-info/competition-results/Results_24.pdf |website=[[European Gymnastics]] |access-date=11 June 2025}}</ref> Then at the [[1985 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships|1985 World Championships]], she won a bronze medal on the uneven bars behind [[Gabriele Fähnrich]] and [[Dagmar Kersten]].<ref>{{cite web |title=1985 World Gymnastics Championships Montreal, Canada November 3-10, 1985 |url=https://static.usagym.org/PDFs/Results/worlds_artistic_results_1985.pdf |website=[[USA Gymnastics]] |access-date=11 June 2025}}</ref> She also finished eighth in the all-around and fifth on the balance beam.<ref>{{cite web |title=1985 World Championships Women's AA |url=https://www.gymn-forum.net/Results/Worlds/Women/1985_aa.html |website=Gymn Forum |access-date=11 June 2025 |date=2 February 2004}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=1985 World Championships Women's EF |url=https://www.gymn-forum.net/Results/Worlds/Women/1985_ef.html |website=Gymn Forum |access-date=11 June 2025 |date=2 February 2004}}</ref>

Říčná represented [[Czechoslovakia at the 1988 Summer Olympics]] and helped the [[Gymnastics at the 1988 Summer Olympics – Women's artistic team all-around|team]] finish seventh.<ref>{{cite web |title=Seoul 1988 Gymnastics Artistic team competition women Results |url=https://www.olympics.com/en/olympic-games/seoul-1988/results/gymnastics-artistic/team-competition-women |website=[[International Olympic Committee]] |access-date=11 June 2025}}</ref> She qualified for the [[Gymnastics at the 1988 Summer Olympics – Women's artistic individual all-around|individual all-around final]] and finished 29th.<ref>{{cite web |title=Seoul 1988 Gymnastics Artistic individual all-round women Results |url=https://www.olympics.com/en/olympic-games/seoul-1988/results/gymnastics-artistic/individual-all-round-women |website=[[International Olympic Committee]] |access-date=11 June 2025}}</ref>

== Post-gymnastics ==
Říčná moved to the United States in 1994 and began coaching gymnastics. She married Lorin Jessen, who is also a gymnastics coach. Her son, [[David Jessen]], is an elite gymnast who represented the [[Czech Republic]] at the [[2016 Summer Olympics]].<ref>{{cite news|title=Interview: David Jessen (Czech Republic)|url=http://www.intlgymnast.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=4754:interview-david-jessen-czech-republicstanford&catid=3:interviews&Itemid=56|publisher=[[International Gymnast Magazine]]|date=15 February 2017|accessdate=15 April 2017|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170705103443/http://www.intlgymnast.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=4754:interview-david-jessen-czech-republicstanford&catid=3:interviews&Itemid=56|archive-date=5 July 2017}}</ref><ref name="medschool">{{cite web |title=Temple/St. Luke's Med Student is Former Olympic Gymnast |url=https://sauconsource.com/2024/07/25/temple-st-lukes-med-student-is-former-olympic-gymnast/ |website=Saucon Source |access-date=1 April 2025 |date=25 July 2024}}</ref> Her daughter Sandra also competed in gymnastics for [[Stanford Cardinal women's gymnastics|Stanford]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Hixson |first1=Chuck |title=Jessen joins older brother at Stanford |url=https://www.lvpnews.com/20191126/jessen-joins-older-brother-at-stanford/ |website=Lehigh Valley Press |access-date=31 March 2025 |date=26 November 2019}}</ref> In 2025, she opened the Ricna Elite Gymnastics Academy in [[Hazleton, Pennsylvania]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Adams |first1=Gregory |title=Olympian opens gymnastics academy in West Hazleton |url=https://www.standardspeaker.com/2025/06/04/olympian-opens-gymnastics-academy-in-west-hazleton/ |website=Hazleton Standard Speaker |access-date=11 June 2025 |date=4 June 2025}}</ref>
