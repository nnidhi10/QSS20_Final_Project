{{Shooting at the 2016 Summer Olympics}}
This article details the qualifying phase for '''[[shooting at the 2016 Summer Olympics]]'''. 366 quota places for the Games are entitled to the shooters coming from their respective NOCs, based on the results at designated ISSF supervised Championships subjected to the ISSF rules from August 1, 2014, to March 31, 2016, while the remaining twenty-four are available to the eligible NOCs under the Tripartite Commission Invitation to get a total quota of 390. Host nation [[Brazil at the 2016 Summer Olympics|Brazil]] has been guaranteed nine quota places with one in each of the following events: 50 m rifle prone, 25 m rapid fire pistol, 10 m air pistol (both men and women), 10 m air rifle (women), trap (both men and women), and skeet (both men and women).<ref name="shooting qual"/><ref name=wch-shooting>{{cite news|title=Rio 2016 Quota Places: the distribution rules|url=http://www.issf-sports.org/news.ashx?newsid=2002|publisher=[[International Shooting Sport Federation|ISSF]]|date=5 February 2014|accessdate=21 October 2015}}</ref>

Quota places can be obtained at the [[2014 ISSF World Shooting Championships|2014]] and 2015 ISSF World Championships, the [[2015 ISSF World Cup]] series, and the designated Continental Championships or Games during the qualifying period.<ref name=wch-shooting/>

==Notes==
*{{note label|IND|a|a}} Sanjeev Rajput secured a second quota place for India in the men's 50 m rifle positions, but the slot was occupied by Gagan Narang as a double starter based on his cumulative scores at the national selection trials. As a result, the NRAI decided to exchange it with an additional men's trap spot, which was awarded to Manavjit Singh Sandhu.<ref>{{cite news|title=Manavjit Singh Sandhu in for Sanjeev Rajput in Indian shooting team for Rio Olympic Games|url=http://indianexpress.com/article/sports/sport-others/manavjit-singh-sandhuin-for-rajput-in-indian-shooting-team-for-rio-olympic-games/|work=[[The Indian Express]]|date=19 March 2016|accessdate=19 March 2016}}</ref>
*{{note label|CHN|b|b}} A total of eighteen shooters were provisionally named to the Chinese team, including two double starters, at the end of Olympic trials for rifle and shooting. As per ISSF rules, China had decided to exchange one of them with the men's 50 m rifle prone spot, which was awarded to Zhao Shengbo. Hence, the remaining berth was returned to ISSF for reallocation.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://sports.sina.com.cn/others/shoot/2016-03-31/doc-ifxqxcnr5097938.shtml|title=射击队17人奥运阵容确定 下月奔赴里约战世界杯|trans-title=A team of 17 shooters will go to the Olympics|language=zh|work=[[Sina Weibo]]|date=31 March 2016|accessdate=9 April 2016}}</ref>
*{{note label|BRA|c|c}} Host nation Brazil had been awarded a minimum of nine quota places in select events with only six shooters using the vacancies for the Games. Almeida Wu and Rippel attained a direct nomination to the Olympic team to leave the vacancies unused for reallocation. With a lack of shooters in women's 10 m air pistol, the Brazilian Shooting Federation decided to exchange it with the men's 50 m pistol, which was awarded to Júlio Almeida.<ref>{{cite news|title=Julio Almeida completa seleção brasileira de tiro nos Jogos do Rio|trans-title=Julio Almeida completes the Brazil selection for Rio|language=pt|url=http://globoesporte.globo.com/olimpiadas/noticia/2016/04/julio-almeida-completa-selecao-brasileira-de-tiro-nos-jogos-do-rio.html|work=[[Globo Esporte]]|date=23 April 2016|accessdate=24 April 2016}}</ref>
*{{note label|SVK|d|d}} Slovakia secured a quota place in the women's trap at the 2015 European Shooting Championships, but Zuzana Štefečeková withdrew from the Games due to her pregnancy. Instead, Slovak Olympic Committee chose to exchange it with an additional spot in its counterpart based on performances throughout the qualifying period. The slot was occupied by Marián Kovačócy.<ref>{{cite news|title=Strelec Kovačócy obsadil v trape na olympijskej strelnici 5. priečku|trans-title=Shooter Kovačócy conquers the Olympic shooting range in men's trap with a fifth place finish|language=sk|url=http://www.teraz.sk/sport/strelec-kovacocy-postupil-v-riu-do-se/192273-clanok.html|publisher=Teraz.sk|date=17 April 2016|accessdate=14 May 2016}}</ref>
*{{note label|SWE|e|e}} Sweden secured a quota place in the women's 10 m air rifle at the 2015 ISSF World Cup meet in Changwon, but the Swedish Olympic Committee chose to exchange it with the men's double trap instead based on performances throughout the qualifying period. The slot was occupied by London 2012 silver medalist Håkan Dahlby.<ref>{{cite news|title=Håkan Dahlby klar för OS Rio|trans-title=Håkan Dahlby is ready for Rio Olympics|language=sv|url=http://na.se/sporten/sporttt/1.3826521-hakan-dahlby-klar-for-os-rio|publisher=[[Nerikes Allehanda]]|date=2 May 2016|accessdate=2 May 2016|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160824203603/http://na.se/sporten/sporttt/1.3826521-hakan-dahlby-klar-for-os-rio|archivedate=24 August 2016}}</ref>
*{{note label|QAT|f|f}} Qatar secured a quota place in the men's 50 m rifle 3 positions at the 2016 Asian Olympic Qualifying Tournament in Delhi, but the Qatar Olympic Committee chose to exchange it with an additional spot in the men's skeet instead based on performances throughout the qualifying period. The slot was occupied by five-time Olympian and London 2012 bronze medalist Nasser Al-Attiyah.<ref>{{cite news|title=Barshim, Al Attiyah to lead Qatar's gold quest in Rio|url=http://thepeninsulaqatar.com/sports/qatar-sport/379922/barshim-al-attiyah-to-lead-qatar-s-gold-quest-in-rio|work=[[The Peninsula (newspaper)|The Peninsula]]|date=27 April 2016|accessdate=30 April 2016}}</ref>
*{{note label|AUS|g|g}} Australia secured a quota place in the women's 50 m rifle 3 positions at the 2016 Oceania Championships in Sydney, but Shooting Australia, in conjunction with the Australian Olympic Committee, chose to exchange it with the women's skeet instead based on performances throughout the qualifying period and selection trials. The slot was occupied by Aislin Jones.<ref>{{cite news|title=Shooting team on target for Rio success|url=http://rio2016.olympics.com.au/news/shooting-team-on-target-for-rio-success|publisher=[[Australian Olympic Committee]]|date=8 April 2016|accessdate=8 April 2016}}</ref>
