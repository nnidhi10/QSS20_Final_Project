'''Rachel Nicol''' (born February 16, 1993)<ref>{{Cite web|title=Rachel Nicol|url=https://olympic.ca/team-canada/rachel-nicol/|access-date=2021-06-13|website=Team Canada - Official Olympic Team Website|date=9 April 2015 |language=en-US}}</ref> is a retired Canadian competitive [[Swimming (sport)|swimmer]], who competed primarily in the [[breaststroke]] events. Nicol won multiple medals for Canada in her career, highlighted by two gold medals at the [[2023 Pan American Games]] in [[Santiago]], [[Chile]], as well as a bronze medal at the [[2022 World Aquatics Championships]].<ref name=Nationalprofile/>

==Career==
In 2016, she qualified for [[Canada at the 2016 Summer Olympics|the Canadian National team]] for the [[2016 Summer Olympics]] in the 100m breaststroke after setting a new personal best of 1:06.88.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.swimming.ca/en/news/2016/04/10/swimming-canada-team-nominated-rio-2016-2/ |title=Olympic Team Nominated for Rio 2016 |date=April 10, 2016 |website=Swimming Canada |accessdate=April 27, 2016}}</ref> At those Olympics in Rio, she finished 5th in the 100m breaststroke with a time of 1:06.68.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rio2016.com/en/swimming-standings-sw-womens-100m-breaststroke |title=Women's 100m Breaststroke |publisher=[[Rio 2016]] |access-date=5 August 2016 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160922161106/https://www.rio2016.com/en/swimming-standings-sw-womens-100m-breaststroke |archive-date=22 September 2016 }}</ref> Nicol was also part of Canada's 4 x 100m medley relay team, and they finished 5th.<ref>{{cite web |title=Rio 2016Swimming 4x100m medley relay women Results |url=https://olympics.com/en/olympic-games/rio-2016/results/swimming/4x100m-medley-relay-women |website=Olympics.com |access-date=1 February 2024}}</ref>

In September 2017, Nicol was named to [[Canada at the 2018 Commonwealth Games|Canada's]] [[2018 Commonwealth Games]] team.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://swimming.ca/en/news/2017/09/26/swimming-canada-nominates-26-athletes-canadas-2018-commonwealth-games-team/ |title=Swimming Canada Nominates 26 Athletes to Canada's 2018 Commonwealth Games Team |date=September 26, 2017 |website=www.swimming.ca/ |publisher=[[Swimming Canada]] |access-date=September 27, 2017}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cbc.ca/sports/olympics/summer/aquatics/oleksiak-masse-canada-commonwealth-games-1.4307648 |title=Oleksiak, Masse headline Canadian swim team for Commonwealth Games |date=September 26, 2017 |website=www.cbc.ca/ |publisher=[[Canadian Broadcasting Corporation]] (CBC) |access-date=September 27, 2017}}</ref>

Nicol medaled at the [[2022 World Aquatics Championships]] as part of the Canadian team in the [[Swimming at the 2022 World Aquatics Championships – Women's 4 × 100 metre medley relay|4×100 m medley relay]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.swimming.ca/en/news/2022/06/25/summer-mcintosh-wins-record-second-gold-fourth-medal-as-canada-completes-best-ever-performance/ |title=Summer McIntosh wins record second gold, fourth medal as Canada completes best-ever performance |date=June 25, 2022 |website=[[Swimming Canada]] |access-date=June 26, 2022}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Canada's Summer McIntosh, 15, wins 2nd gold medal at world aquatics|url=https://www.cbc.ca/sports/olympics/summer/aquatics/swimming/world-aquatics-roundup-june-25-1.6501660|last=Harrison|first=Doug|website=[[CBC Sports]]|date=June 25, 2022|access-date=June 26, 2022}}</ref>

Nicol won two gold medals at the [[2023 Pan American Games]] in [[Santiago]], [[Chile]], where she won the [[Swimming at the 2023 Pan American Games – Women's 100 metre breaststroke|100 m breaststroke event]],<ref>{{cite web |last1=Paula Nichols |first1=Michael Charlebois |title=Day 1 at Santiago 2023: Canada sweeps mountain bike, earns two golds in swimming |url=https://olympic.ca/2023/10/21/day-1-at-santiago-2023-canada-sweeps-mountain-bike-earns-two-golds-in-swimming/ |website=Canadian Olympic Committee |access-date=1 February 2024}}</ref> and helped Canada win [[Swimming at the 2023 Pan American Games – Women's 4 × 100 metre medley relay|the 4 x 100 medley relay]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Fenton |first1=Caela |title=Day 5 at Santiago 2023: Mac Neil makes history, badminton gold rush |url=https://olympic.ca/2023/10/25/day-5-at-santiago-2023-mac-neil-makes-history-badminton-gold-rush/ |website=Canadian Olympic Committee |access-date=1 February 2024}}</ref>

==Retirement and personal life==

Nicol announced her retirement from swimming in January 2024.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Swimming Canada |title=Some things mean more than medals as Nicol reflects on retirement |url=https://sirc.ca/news/some-things-mean-more-than-medals-as-nicol-reflects-on-retiremen/ |website=SIRC |access-date=1 February 2024}}</ref>

She completed an undergraduate degree in applied physiology and sport management at Southern Methodist University, and then a masters degree in kinesiology at the University of Calgary (2022).<ref>{{cite web |title=Rachel Nicol |url=https://olympic.ca/team-canada/rachel-nicol/ |website=Canadian Olympic Committee |access-date=1 February 2024}}</ref>
