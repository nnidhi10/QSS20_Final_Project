The '''Men's [[470 (dinghy)|470]]''' was a sailing event on the [[Sailing at the 2004 Summer Olympics]] program in [[Agios Kosmas Olympic Sailing Centre]],<ref name="A" />  in the [[470 (dinghy)|470 dinghy]]. Eleven races were scheduled and completed. 54 sailors, on 27 boats, from 27 nation competed.<ref name="Scoring_System" /><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/11012 |title=Sailing at the 2004 Summer Olympics: Two Person Dinghy (470), Men |work=Olympedia |access-date=29 June 2020}}</ref>
