The first '''world record in the 100&nbsp;metres [[butterfly stroke|butterfly]]''' in long course (50&nbsp;metres) [[swimming (sport)|swimming]] was recognised by the [[International Swimming Federation]] (FINA) in [[1957 in sports|1957]], for both men and women. In the short-course (25&nbsp;metres) swimming events, the world's governing body recognizes world records since March 3, 1991.
