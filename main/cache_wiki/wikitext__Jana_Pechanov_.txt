'''Jana Pechanová''' ({{IPA|cs|ˈjana ˈpɛxanovaː}}; born 3 March 1981<ref>{{in lang|cs}} [http://www.sportovci.cz/plavani/pechanova-jana/ Pechanová's entry] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110716104243/http://www.sportovci.cz/plavani/pechanova-jana/ |date=2011-07-16 }} from sportovci.cz; retrieved 2010-02-20.</ref> in [[Rakovník|Rakovník, Czechoslovakia]])<ref name=":0">[https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/pe/jana-pechanova-1.html Pechanová's entry] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121025075725/http://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/pe/jana-pechanova-1.html |date=2012-10-25 }} at sports-reference.com; retrieved 2010-02-20.</ref> is an Olympic distance swimmer from the [[Czech Republic]]. She swam for the Czech Olympic team at the [[Czech Republic at the 2004 Summer Olympics|2004]], [[Czech Republic at the 2008 Summer Olympics|2008]], [[Czech Republic at the 2012 Summer Olympics|2012]] and [[Czech Republic at the 2016 Summer Olympics|2016]].

At the 2004 Summer Olympics, she in 19th in the 10 km open water marathon.<ref name=":0" />  At the 2008 Summer Olympics, Pechanová finished 8th in [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's marathon 10 kilometre|10&nbsp;km distance]].  At the 2012 Summer Olympics, she finished 9th in the [[Swimming at the 2008 Summer Olympics – Women's marathon 10 kilometre|same event]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.olympic.org/olympic-results/london-2012/swimming/marathon-10km-w|title=London 2012 Marathon - 10 km women Results - Olympic marathon-swimming}}</ref>
