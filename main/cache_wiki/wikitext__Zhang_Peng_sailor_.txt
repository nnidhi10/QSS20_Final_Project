'''Zhang Peng''' (born 1981-06-04 in [[Qingdao]], [[Shandong]]) is a male [[PR China|Chinese]] sports [[sailing (sport)|sailor]] who competed for [[China at the 2008 Summer Olympics|Team China]] at the [[2008 Summer Olympics]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://2008teamchina.olympic.cn/index.php/personview/personsen/1797|title=BEIJING 2008 OLYMPIC GAMES CHINESE SPORTS DELEGATION ROSTER|website=2008teamchina.olympic.cn|access-date=2016-05-04|archive-date=2016-03-03|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303230835/http://2008teamchina.olympic.cn/index.php/personview/personsen/1797|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Peng placed 24th in the Finn Heavyweight Dinghy Mixed event.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Olympic Games Beijing 2008 |url=https://olympics.com/en/olympic-games/beijing-2008/results/sailing/finn-heavyweight-dinghy-mixed}}</ref>

==Major performances==
*2001 National Games – 2nd Finn class;
*2002/2005/2006/2007 National Championships – 1st Finn class;
*2005/2007/2008 National Champions Tournament – 1st Finn class
