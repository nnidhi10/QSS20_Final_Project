The men's 1000 metres in speed skating at the 1994 Winter Olympics took place on 18 February, at the Hamar Olympic Hall.[1][2]

Records

Prior to this competition, the existing world and Olympic records were as follows:[3][4]

World record  Kevin Scott (CAN)1:12.54 Calgary, Canada17 December 1993
Olympic record  Nikolai Gulyayev (URS)1:13.03 Calgary, Canada18 February 1988
