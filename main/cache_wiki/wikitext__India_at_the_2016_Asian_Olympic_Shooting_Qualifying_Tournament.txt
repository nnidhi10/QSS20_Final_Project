A total of 64 Indian shooters participated in the [[2016 Asian Olympic Shooting Qualifying Tournament]] held in Dr. Karni Singh Shooting Range, [[New Delhi]].<ref>{{Cite web|title = Asia Olympic Qualifying Competition is about to start in New Delhi|url = https://www.issf-sports.org/news.ashx?newsid=2461|website = ISSF|access-date = 2016-02-01}}</ref> India ranked 5th in the medal list.<ref>{{Cite web|title = UAE's Saif Bin Futtais secures the Skeet Men Gold in the closing event of the Asia Olympic Qualifying Competition|url = https://www.issf-sports.org/news.ashx?newsid=2480|website = ISSF|access-date = 2016-02-02}}</ref>

A total of 4 quotas have been obtained in three different events till 1 February.
