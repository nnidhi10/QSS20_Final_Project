'''Rick van der Ven''' (born 14 April 1991 in [[Oss]])<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.london2012.com/athlete/van-der-ven-rick-1104827/|title=Rick van der Ven – Archery – Olympic Athlete|publisher=2012 London Olympic Summer Games. International Olympic Committee|accessdate=2 August 2012|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20120822034658/http://www.london2012.com/athlete/van-der-ven-rick-1104827/|archivedate=22 August 2012}}</ref> is a Dutch Olympic [[archery|archer]] who started to practise archery at the age of 8.

In 2009 at the World Junior Championship in [[Ogden, Utah]], Rick was the runner-up with a silver medal. In 2011 at the World Junior Championship in [[Legnica]], [[Poland]] he won silver with the Mixed team, bronze with the men's team and finished 4th in the individual event. In May 2012 in the [[Olympic Stadium (Amsterdam)|Olympic Stadium]] in [[Amsterdam]], Van der Ven became European Champion in Outdoor Archery, both individually and in the team event. In February 2013, in [[Rzeszów]], Van der Ven became also European Champion Indoor, again individually as well as with the Dutch team.

{{Update section|date=December 2020}}

==2012 Olympic Games==
Van der Ven competed at the [[2012 Summer Olympics]] in London in [[Archery at the 2012 Summer Olympics – Men's individual|men's individual archery]]. He was coached by [[Wietse van Alten]], winner of the bronze medal in Sydney 2000.<ref name=sref>{{cite Sports-Reference |title=Rick van der Ven |url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/va/rick-van-der-ven-1.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200418112331/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/va/rick-van-der-ven-1.html |url-status=dead |archive-date=18 April 2020 |accessdate=30 July 2012}}</ref> In an upset victory, he convincingly beat top-seed and world-record holder [[Im Dong-hyun]] 7–1 in the round of 16.<ref>[http://www.london2012.com/news/articles/van-der-ven-ousts-top-seed.html Van der Ven ousts top seed] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130123095344/http://www.london2012.com/news/articles/van-der-ven-ousts-top-seed.html |date=23 January 2013 }} at www.london2012.com</ref> After beating [[Kuo Cheng-Wei]] 6–0 in the quarterfinals, he came to a 5–5 draw in the semi-finals against [[Takaharu Furukawa]], shooting 141 versus Furukawa's 140.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.london2012.com/archery/event/men-individual/match=arm070201/index.html|title=London 2012 individual FITA Olympic round 70m men Results – Olympic archery|access-date=5 August 2012|archive-date=5 August 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120805200506/http://www.london2012.com/archery/event/men-individual/match=arm070201/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> In the following shoot-off he shot a 9 while Furukawa shot a 10, relegating him to a bronze medal match against [[Dai Xiaoxiang]]. Here Van der Ven again came to a 5–5 draw, scoring 2 points more than his opponent, but lost again in the first shoot-off, thereby finishing in 4th position.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.london2012.com/archery/event/men-individual/match=arm070102/index.html|title=London 2012 individual FITA Olympic round 70m men Results – Olympic archery|access-date=5 August 2012|archive-date=27 January 2013|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130127232237/www.london2012.com/archery/event/men-individual/match=arm070102/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>
Since 2009 Rick van der Ven is training at The Olympic Sportcentre in [[Papendal]] and is studying Engineering Mechanics in Arnhem.

== 2016 Olympic Games ==
At the [[2016 Summer Olympics|2016 Olympic Games]], van der Ven competed in both the individual and the men's team events.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|title=Rick van der Ven Bio, Stats, and Results {{!}} Olympics at Sports-Reference.com|url=http://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/va/rick-van-der-ven-1.html|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200114163142/http://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/va/rick-van-der-ven-1.html|archive-date=14 January 2020|access-date=6 December 2020|website=sports-reference.com}}</ref> In the individual event, van der Ven was in 27th after the ranking round, and faced [[Patrick Huston]] in the first knock-out round, losing 4–6.<ref name=":0" /> In the team event, the Netherlands were ranked in 9th after the ranking round. They faced the Spanish team in the first elimination round, winning 5–1, before losing to the South Korean team 0–6.<ref name=":0" />
