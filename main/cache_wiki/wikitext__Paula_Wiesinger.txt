==Biography==
Wiesinger was born in [[Bolzano]]. She won the 1932 women's [[FIS Alpine World Ski Championships|Downhill world championship]] in [[Cortina d'Ampezzo]], and competed in the [[1936 Winter Olympics]], finishing 16th in the [[Alpine skiing at the 1936 Winter Olympics – Women's combined|alpine skiing combined event]]. She married Hans Steger.<ref>[http://www.fembio.org/biographie.php/frau/biographie/paula-wiesinger/ ''Paula Wiesinger''] (German), FemBio.</ref>

In 1935 Wiesinger was invited to view the [[Trofeo Mezzalama]], a competition that was only intended for male [[ski mountaineer]]s. Due to an injury leave of [[Giusto Gervasutti]], she took his military uniform, covered her face with sunglasses and his cap, and took part instead of him, but the cheat was discovered at a check point of the race.<ref>[http://www.fembio.org/biographie.php/frau/biographie/paula-wiesinger/ ''Paula Wiesinger''] (German), FemBio.</ref> She died in [[Seiser Alm]]. A hotel and a statue in [[Siusi allo Sciliar]] are dedicated to Wiesinger.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.alpesiusi.it/lapaula/|title=Paula Wiesinger Apartments & Suites|publisher=alpesiusi.it|accessdate=28 January 2018}}</ref>
