'''Emmanuel "Emma" Lucenti''' (born 23 November 1984) is a [[judoka]] from [[Argentina]].  He competed at the 2008, 2012 and 2016 Summer Olympics in the men's half-middleweight ({{nbnd}}81&nbsp;kg).<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|title=Emmanuel Lucenti Bio, Stats, and Results |url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/lu/emmanuel-lucenti-1.html |website=Olympics at Sports-Reference.com |accessdate=11 September 2015 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150930120531/http://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/lu/emmanuel-lucenti-1.html |archivedate=30 September 2015 }}</ref>  At the [[2008 Summer Olympics]], he lost in second round to [[Euan Burton]].<ref>{{Cite web|title = 73 - 81kg (half-middleweight) men results - Judo - Beijing 2008 Olympics|url = http://www.olympic.org/olympic-results/beijing-2008/judo/73-81kg-half-middleweight-m|website = www.olympic.org|accessdate = 11 September 2015}}</ref>  At the [[2012 Summer Olympics]], he reached the quarter-finals, where he lost to [[Kim Jae-bum]].<ref name=":0" />  As Kim continued on to the gold medal match, Lucenti took part in the bronze medal repechage, where he lost in the first round to [[Antoine Valois-Fortier]].<ref>{{Cite web|title = 73 - 81kg (half-middleweight) men results - Judo - London 2012 Olympics|url = http://www.olympic.org/olympic-results/london-2012/judo/73-81kg-half-middleweight-m|website = www.olympic.org|accessdate = 11 September 2015}}</ref> At the [[2016 Summer Olympics]], Lucenti was once again eliminated by Valois-Fortier, this time in the third round.<ref>{{cite web|title=Emmanuel Lucenti|url=https://www.rio2016.com/en/athlete/emmanuel-lucenti|website=Rio 2016|accessdate=16 August 2016|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160817030547/https://www.rio2016.com/en/athlete/emmanuel-lucenti|archivedate=17 August 2016}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Men's -81 kg - Standings|url=https://www.rio2016.com/en/judo-standings-ju-men-81-kg|website=Rio 2016|accessdate=16 August 2016|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160815024027/https://www.rio2016.com/en/judo-standings-ju-men-81-kg|archivedate=15 August 2016}}</ref>

Lucenti qualified to represent [[Argentina at the 2020 Summer Olympics]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Diario Democracia|url=https://www.diariodemocracia.com/mas-deportivo/polideportivo/242525-tucumano-emmanuel-lucenti-logro-ranking-plaza-ir-t/|access-date=27 June 2021 |website=Diario Democracia|language=es}}</ref>

Lucenti's older brother [[Rodrigo Lucenti|Rodrigo]] was also an Olympic judoka, competing in the lightweight ({{nbnd}}73&nbsp;kg) category.<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite web|title=Rodrigo Lucenti Bio, Stats, and Results |url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/lu/rodrigo-lucenti-1.html |website=Olympics at Sports-Reference.com |accessdate=11 September 2015 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150911033616/http://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/lu/rodrigo-lucenti-1.html |archivedate=11 September 2015 }}</ref>
