Franka Anić (born 5 February 1991 in Split, Croatia)[1] is a Slovenian-Croatian taekwondo athlete. Anić lives in Korčula, Croatia, but competes internationally for Slovenia.[2]

Anić qualified for the 2012 Summer Olympics in the 67 kg category. She reached the semifinals where she lost to Hwang Kyung-Seon. In the bronze medal match, she lost to Paige McPherson, thus finishing in 5th place in the tournament.[3] She was Slovenia's flagbearer during the closing ceremonies.[citation needed]
