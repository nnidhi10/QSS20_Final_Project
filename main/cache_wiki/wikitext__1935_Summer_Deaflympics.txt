'''The 1935 Summer Deaflympics''' officially known as the 4th International Games for the Deaf was an international multi-sport event that was held from 17 August 1935 to 24 August 1935. It was hosted by [[London]], [[England]],<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.deaflympics.com/games.asp?1935-s|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130704092349/http://www.deaflympics.com/games.asp?1935-s|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 4, 2013|title=Games {{!}} Deaflympics|website=www.deaflympics.com|language=en|access-date=2017-08-28}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://deaflympics2017.org/en/history-of-deaflympics-detay/44|title=Deaflympics 2017 Samsun|website=deaflympics2017.org|language=tr|access-date=2017-09-02}}</ref> with events held at [[White City Stadium]]. It was the only parasport event to be held in the [[United Kingdom]] until the [[2012 Summer Paralympics]] as part of the [[Paralympic Games]].<ref>John R. Gold and Margaret M. Gold, ''Olympic Cities: City Agendas, Planning, and the World's Games, 1896 – 2016'', p.29</ref>

The tournament saw the debut of an American team.<ref>Karen P. DePauw and Susan J. Gavron, ''Disability Sport'' (2nd Edition), p.277</ref>
