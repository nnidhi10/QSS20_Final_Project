'''Erika Ferraioli''' (born 23 March 1986) is an Italian [[Swimming (sport)|swimmer]].

At the [[2008 Summer Olympics]], she was part of the Italian 4 × 100 m freestyle relay team.<ref>{{Cite web |title=4x100m freestyle relay women results - Swimming - Beijing 2008 Olympics |url=http://www.olympic.org/olympic-results/beijing-2008/swimming/4x100m-freestyle-relay-w |website=www.olympic.org |access-date=2016-02-12}}</ref>  She competed in the [[Swimming at the 2012 Summer Olympics – Women's 50 metre freestyle|women's 50m freestyle]] at the [[2012 Summer Olympics]] in London, finishing with a time of 25.69 seconds in 32nd place in the heats, as well as being part of the 4 × 100 m freestyle team.<ref>{{Cite web |title=4x100m freestyle relay women results - Swimming - London 2012 Olympics |url=http://www.olympic.org/olympic-results/london-2012/swimming/4x100m-freestyle-relay-w |website=www.olympic.org |access-date=2016-02-12}}</ref>
