The '''men's 4 × 200 metre freestyle relay''' event at the 1952 Olympic Games took place from 28 to 29 July at the [[Helsinki Swimming Stadium|Swimming Stadium]].<ref name="SportsRef">{{cite web |url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/summer/1952/SWI/mens-4-x-200-metres-freestyle-relay.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200417165953/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/summer/1952/SWI/mens-4-x-200-metres-freestyle-relay.html |url-status=dead |archive-date=17 April 2020 |title=Swimming at the 1952 Helsinki Summer Games: Men's 4 × 200 metres Freestyle Relay |accessdate=28 September 2016 |work=Sports Reference}}</ref> The relay featured teams of four swimmers each [[Swimming (sport)|swimming]] four lengths of the 50&nbsp;m pool [[Freestyle swimming|freestyle]].
