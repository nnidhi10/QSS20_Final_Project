'''Alan James "Al" Wills''' (born 3 August 1981 in [[Cumbria]]) is an athlete from the United Kingdom.  He competes in [[archery]].<ref>{{cite Sports-Reference |title=Al Wills |url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/wi/al-wills-1.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161204044345/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/wi/al-wills-1.html |archive-date=4 December 2016}}</ref>

==2008 Summer Olympics==
Wills represented [[Great Britain at the 2008 Summer Olympics]] in the [[Archery at the 2008 Summer Olympics]], competing in the men's individual and team archery. He finished his ranking round with a total of 661 points. This gave him the 21st seed for the final competition bracket. There he beat [[Mauro Nespoli]] 103–98 in the first round and went on to edge out another Italian, [[Marco Galiazzo]], 110–109 in round two. His run was ended by [[Juan Carlos Stevens]] of [[Cuba]] in the last 16 as he was defeated 108–104.<ref name="beijing">{{cite web |url=http://results.beijing2008.cn/WRM/ENG/BIO/Athlete/4/224904.shtml |title=Athlete biography: Alan Wills |website=Beijing2008.cn |publisher=[[The Beijing Organizing Committee for the Games of the XXIX Olympiad]] |access-date=17 August 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080814012316/http://results.beijing2008.cn/WRM/ENG/BIO/Athlete/4/224904.shtml |archive-date=14 August 2008}}</ref>

Together with [[Simon Terry]] and [[Laurence Godfrey (archer)|Laurence Godfrey]] he also took part in the team event. With his 661 score from the ranking round combined with the 670 of Simon Terry and the 657 of Godfrey the British were in fifth position after the ranking round. In the first elimination round they faced the [[China|Chinese]] team and got eliminated with 214–210. The Chinese went on to win the bronze medal.<ref name="beijing"/>

==2012 Summer Olympics==

At the [[2012 Summer Olympics]], Wills again competed in both the men's individual and team archery competitions.  In the individual, he was knocked out in the first round by [[Taylor Worth]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.olympic.org/olympic-results/london-2012/archery/individual-fita-olympic-round-70m-m |title=London 2012 individual FITA Olympic round 70m men Results – Olympic archery |website=Olympic.org |publisher=[[International Olympic Committee]] |access-date=20 October 2013 |archive-date=25 May 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160525220241/http://www.olympic.org/olympic-results/london-2012/archery/individual-fita-olympic-round-70m-m |url-status=dead}}</ref> The British team were knocked out by Ukraine in the first round of the team event.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.olympic.org/olympic-results/london-2012/archery/team-fita-olympic-round-70m-m |title=London 2012 team FITA Olympic round 70m men Results – Olympic archery |website=Olympic.org |publisher=International Olympic Committee |access-date=20 October 2013 |archive-date=4 February 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160204123521/http://www.olympic.org/olympic-results/london-2012/archery/team-fita-olympic-round-70m-m |url-status=dead}}</ref>
