{{Snowboarding at the 2018 Winter Olympics}}
The '''men's [[big air]]''' competition of the [[2018 Winter Olympics]] was held on 21 and 24 February 2018 at the [[Alpensia Ski Jumping Stadium]] in [[Pyeongchang]], [[South Korea]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.pyeongchang2018.com/en/schedule |title=Schedule |access-date=2018-01-05 |archive-date=2017-11-05 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171105010048/https://www.pyeongchang2018.com/en/schedule |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>[http://data.fis-ski.com/pdf/2018/SB/7081/2018SB7081SLH1.pdf Start list]</ref> The event was making its Olympic debut.

Due to a broken shoulder caused by a crash in training for the slopestyle, [[Niek van der Velden]] was not able to compete.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://nos.nl/pyeongchang2018/artikel/2216241-valse-start-nederland-snowboarder-van-der-velden-haakt-af.html |title=Valse start Nederland: snowboarder Van der Velden haakt af |publisher=[[Nederlandse Omroep Stichting|NOS]] |access-date=2018-02-10 |archive-date=2018-06-13 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180613085815/https://nos.nl/pyeongchang2018/artikel/2216241-valse-start-nederland-snowboarder-van-der-velden-haakt-af.html |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nu.nl/olympische-winterspelen-pyeongchang-2018/5127017/einde-spelen-snowboarder-van-velden-breuk-val.html|title=Einde Spelen voor snowboarder van der Velden door breuk na val|date=10 February 2018}}</ref>

==Qualification==
{{main|Snowboarding at the 2018 Winter Olympics – Qualification}}
The top 40 athletes in the Olympic quota allocation list qualified (for both big air and slopestyle, the rankings were combined). This meant only a maximum of 40 could qualify across both events. A maximum of four athletes per [[National Olympic Committee]] (NOC) was allowed. All athletes qualifying must also have placed in the top 30 of a FIS World Cup event (in either big air or slopestyle) or the [[FIS Freestyle Ski and Snowboarding World Championships 2017]] during the qualification period (1 July 2016 to 21 January 2018) and also have a minimum of 50 FIS points to compete. If the host country, [[South Korea at the 2018 Winter Olympics|South Korea]], did not qualify, their chosen athlete would displace the last qualified athlete, granted all qualification criterion was met.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://data.fis-ski.com/media/olympic-games/2018/english-qs/owg-pyeongchang-2018-qualification-system-snowboard.pdf|title=Qualification Systems for XXII Olympic Winter Games, PyeongChang 2018 Snowboarding|date=9 September 2016|publisher=[[International Ski Federation]] (FIS)|accessdate=21 January 2018}}</ref>
