{{Freestyle skiing at the 2002 Winter Olympics}}
The '''Men's Aerials''' event in '''[[freestyle skiing]]''' at the '''[[2002 Winter Olympics]]''' in [[Salt Lake City]], [[United States]] took place on 16 and 19 February at [[Park City, Utah|Park City]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympedia.org/results/1849 |title=Freestyle Skiing at the 2002 Winter Olympics: Aerials, Men |work=Olympedia |accessdate=9 July 2020}}</ref>

==Results==
===Qualification===
The qualification was held on 16 February at 13:30.<ref>[http://medias4.fis-ski.com/pdf/2002/FS/8156/8156QRL.pdf Qualification results]</ref> The top 12 advanced to the final.
