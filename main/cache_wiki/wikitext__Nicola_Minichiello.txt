'''Nicola Minichiello''' (born '''Nicola Gautier''' on 21 March 1978) is a retired [[Great Britain|British]] [[bobsled]]der who competed between 2001 and 2011. She won two medals in the two-woman event at the [[FIBT World Championships]], winning a silver in [[FIBT World Championships 2005|2005]] and making history with a gold in [[FIBT World Championships 2009|2009]] partnering [[Gillian Cooke]], to become the first British female bobsleigh driver to win a World Championships. Competing in three [[Winter Olympics]], Minichiello earned her best finish of ninth in the two-woman event at [[Turin]] in [[2006 Winter Olympics|2006]]. This was also the best ever Olympic result by a GB women’s bobsleigh team.

Before taking up bobsleigh, Minichiello had competed (under her maiden name of Gautier) for Sheffield Athletic Club in [[shot put]], [[javelin]] and [[heptathlon]]. Her lifetime best for the heptathlon was 5784 points, which she achieved in Austria in 2001.<ref>{{cite web |url= http://www.thepowerof10.info/athletes/profile.aspx?athleteid=40863 |title= Nicola Gautier profile |publisher= thepowerof10.info |accessdate= 16 November 2012}}</ref> While competing in athletics, she met and married [[Toni Minichiello]], coach of World and Olympic [[heptathlon]] champion [[Jessica Ennis]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.telegraph.co.uk/sport/othersports/winter-olympics/7083079/Fire-and-ice-British-women-go-for-bobsleigh-gold-at-Winter-Olympics-in-Vancouver.html |title=Fire and ice: British women go for bobsleigh gold at Winter Olympics in Vancouver |first=Chris |last=Harvey|date=28 January 2012|work=[[Telegraph.co.uk]]|accessdate=21 June 2012}}</ref> Nicola was involved in coaching Ennis during Ennis' early years in athletics.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/general/athletics/meet-minichiello-the-big-man-behind-enniss-grand-plan-2328473.html |archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/20220620/https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/general/athletics/meet-minichiello-the-big-man-behind-enniss-grand-plan-2328473.html |archive-date=20 June 2022 |url-access=subscription |url-status=live |title=Meet Minichiello: The big man behind Ennis's grand plan |last1=Turnbull |first1=Simon |date=30 July 2011 |website=[[independent.co.uk]]  |accessdate=28 September 2014}}</ref> When not bobsledding, Minichiello worked as a part-time P.E. teacher and also as an athlete mentor for the Youth Sport trust. It was announced on 10 August 2010 that Minichiello would miss the 2010-11 Bobsleigh World Cup, including the [[FIBT World Championships 2011]], due to a knee injury. Subsequently, in April 2011 Minichiello announced her retirement as a driver and took up a position as head development coach at the sport's governing body, the [[International Bobsleigh & Skeleton Federation]] (French: ''Fédération Internationale de Bobsleigh et de Tobogganing'').<ref name="retirement">{{cite web |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/winter-sports/13120354 |title=Bobsleigher Nicola Minichiello announces retirement|date=18 April 2011|publisher=[[BBC.co.uk]]|access-date=10 March 2012}}</ref>

In August 2012, she became the head of performance for the Netherlands Olympic Bobsleigh team, becoming the first woman to head a Winter Olympic sports organisation.<ref>{{cite news | url= https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/winter-sports/18157927 | title= Nicola Minichiello aims to change 'old-school' bobsleigh attitudes | publisher= BBC.co.uk | date= 22 May 2012}}</ref> In this role she coached [[Esmé Kamphuis]] and [[Judith Vis]] to fourth place in the [[Bobsleigh at the 2014 Winter Olympics – Two-woman|two-woman]] event at the [[2014 Winter Olympics]] in [[Sochi]], [[Russia]]. In September 2014 she was announced as the FIBT's new coordinator for North American races, with responsibility for bobsleigh and skeleton races on the North American Cup and [[Intercontinental Cup (skeleton)|Intercontinental Cup]] in Canada and the United States.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ibsf.org/en/news/9-skeleton/19053-nicola-minichiello-appointed-new-fibt-coordinator-for-north-american-races |title=Nicola Minichiello appointed new FIBT coordinator for North American races |author=<!--Not stated-->|date=3 September 2014 |website=[[International Bobsleigh and Skeleton Federation]] |access-date=10 March 2018}}</ref>
