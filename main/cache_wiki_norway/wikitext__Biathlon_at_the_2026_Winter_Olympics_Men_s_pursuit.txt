{{Biathlon at the 2026 Winter Olympics}}
The '''men's pursuit''' competition of the [[Biathlon at the 2026 Winter Olympics|2026 Winter Olympics]] was held on 15 February,<!-- 2026--> at the [[South Tyrol Arena|Anterselva Biathlon Arena]] in [[Rasen-Antholz]].<ref name="schedule">{{cite web|url=https://www.biathlonworld.com/calendar?SeasonId=2526&CupLevel=1&EventId=BT2526SWRLOG__|title=2026 Olympic Games – Antholz-Anterselva|publisher=[[International Biathlon Union|IBU]]|access-date=13 February 2026}}</ref> [[Martin Ponsiluoma]] of Sweden won the event, his first Olympic gold. [[Sturla Holm Lægreid]] of Norway won the silver medal, and [[Émilien Jacquelin]] of France won bronze, his first individual Olympic medal.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.espn.co.uk/olympics/story/_/id/47936484/sweden-martin-ponsiluoma-wins-olympic-gold-pursuit-biathlon|title=Sweden's Martin Ponsiluoma wins Olympic gold in pursuit biathlon|publisher=ESPN|accessdate=27 February 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://apnews.com/article/biathlon-olympics-pursuit-a2932229ef38d8c32bcf8aa8f5f251dc|title=Martin Ponsiluoma secures Olympic gold for Sweden in pursuit biathlon race|publisher=APNews|accessdate=27 February 2026}}</ref>

==Background==
The pursuit is contingent on the results of the sprint held two days earlier. [[Quentin Fillon Maillet]], the winner of the sprint, and also the defending champion in pursuit, started first, followed by the silver medalist [[Vetle Sjåstad Christiansen]] in 14 seconds and [[Sturla Holm Lægreid]] (3rd) and [[Émilien Jacquelin]] (4th) in 16 seconds. The 2022 silver medalist [[Tarjei Bø]] retired from competitions, and the bronze medalist [[Eduard Latypov]] was banned from participation because of the Russian invasion of Ukraine. Prior to the Olympics, [[Éric Perrot]] was leading the total as well as the pursuit standings of the [[2025–26 Biathlon World Cup]]. The [[Biathlon World Championships 2025 – Men's pursuit|2025 World champion]] was [[Johannes Thingnes Bø]], he retired from competitions in the meanwhile.
