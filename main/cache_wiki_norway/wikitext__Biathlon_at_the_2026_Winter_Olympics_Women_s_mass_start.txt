{{Biathlon at the 2026 Winter Olympics}}
The '''women's mass start''' competition of the [[Biathlon at the 2026 Winter Olympics|2026 Winter Olympics]] was held on 21 February,<!-- 2026--> at the [[South Tyrol Arena|Anterselva Biathlon Arena]] in [[Rasen-Antholz]].<ref name="schedule">{{cite web|url=https://www.biathlonworld.com/calendar?SeasonId=2526&CupLevel=1&EventId=BT2526SWRLOG__|title=2026 Olympic Games – Antholz-Anterselva|publisher=[[International Biathlon Union|IBU]]|access-date=20 February 2026}}</ref> [[Océane Michelon]] of France won the event, thus securing her first individual Olympic gold. Her teammate [[Julia Simon (biathlete)|Julia Simon]] won the silver medal, and [[Tereza Voborníková]] of the Czech Republic won bronze, her first Olympic medal.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rfi.fr/en/sports/20260221-france-ocene-michelon-wins-winter-olympics-gold-women-biathlon-mass-start|title=France's Michelon wins Olympic gold in women’s biathlon mass start|publisher=Radio France Internationale|accessdate=27 February 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nbcolympics.com/news/frances-michelon-hits-gold-target-womens-125km-biathlon|title=France's Oceane Michelon hits golden target in women's 12.5km biathlon|publisher=NBC Olympics|accessdate=27 February 2026}}</ref>

==Background==
The defending champion, [[Justine Braisaz-Bouchet]], qualified for the Olympics. The silver and bronze medalists, [[Tiril Eckhoff]] and [[Marte Olsbu Røiseland]], retired from competitions. Prior to the Olympics, [[Lou Jeanmonnot]] was leading the total as well as mass-start standings of the [[2025–26 Biathlon World Cup]]. [[Elvira Öberg]] was the [[Biathlon World Championships 2025 – Women's mass start|2025 World champion]].
