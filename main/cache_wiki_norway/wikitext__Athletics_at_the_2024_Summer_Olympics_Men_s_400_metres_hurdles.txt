The men's [[400 metres hurdles]] at the [[2024 Summer Olympics]] was held in four rounds at the [[Stade de France]] in [[Paris]], France, from 5 to 9 August 2024. This was 28th time that the men's 400 metres hurdles was contested at the [[Summer Olympics]]. A total of 40 athletes were able to qualify for the event by entry standard or ranking.
{{TOC limit|3}}

==Summary==
In the build up to the Games, World Athletics touted [[Athletics at the 2020 Summer Olympics – Men's 400 metres hurdles|reigning Olympic champion]] and world record holder [[Karsten Warholm]], 2020 Olympic silver medallist and world leader [[Rai Benjamin]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://worldathletics.org/records/all-time-toplists/hurdles/400-metres-hurdles/all/men/senior?regionType=world&timing=electronic&page=1&bestResultsOnly=true&firstDay=2024-01-01&lastDay=2024-08-01&maxResultsByCountry=all&eventId=10229612&ageCategory=senior|title=Men's 400 metres hurdles - Toplists - to 1 August 2024|website=World Athletics|access-date=6 September 2025}}</ref> and [[2022 World Athletics Championships – Men's 400 metres hurdles|2022 world champion]] [[Alison Dos Santos]] for a battle for the gold.<ref name="WApreview">{{cite web|url=https://worldathletics.org/competitions/olympic-games/the-xxxiii-olympic-games-7153115/news/article/paris-olympics-preview-400m-hurdles|title=Paris Olympics preview: 400m hurdles|website=World Athletics|date=27 July 2024|access-date=6 September 2025|last=Jeffery|first=Nicole}}</ref> Other athletes seen as medal contenders included 2023 world silver medallist [[Kyron McMaster]], 2023 world fourth-placer [[Roshawn Clarke]] and fellow Jamaican [[Malik James-King]].<ref name=WApreview />

Rain on the day of the final may have precluded the possibility of a world record. In the final, Warholm went out to the fastest start, reaching the third hurdle first in 12.73&nbsp;s.<ref name="Analysis">{{cite web |title=Men's 400m Hurdles - Final race analysis |url=https://olympics.com/OG2024/pdf/OG2024/ATH/OG2024_ATH_C77A_ATHM400MHURD----------FNL-000100--.pdf |website=Olympics |access-date=11 August 2024}}</ref> Clarke and Benjamin following in second (12.77&nbsp;s). By the halfway mark at the fifth hurdle, dos Santos has passed [[Clement Ducos]] for the third placed spot. Benjamin took the lead over the eighth hurdle, with Warholm in second and dos Santos in third. They maintained their positions up until the line.<ref name=Analysis />

==Background==

The men's 400 m hurdles has been present on the [[Athletics at the Summer Olympics|Olympic athletics programme]] since [[1900 Summer Olympics|1900]], with a sole gap at the [[1912 Summer Olympics]].

==Qualification==
{{main|Athletics at the 2024 Summer Olympics – Qualification#Men's 400 m hurdles}}

For the men's 400 metres hurdles event, the qualification period was between 1 July 2023 and 30 June 2024.<ref name="qualification">Sean McAlister, "[https://olympics.com/en/news/how-to-qualify-paris-2024-athletics-qualification-system-explained How to qualify for athletics at Paris 2024. The Olympics qualification system explained]", ''[[Olympics.com]]'', 20 December 2022. Retrieved 29 June 2024.</ref> Forty athletes were able to qualify for the event, with a maximum of three athletes per nation, by running the entry standard of 48.70&nbsp;seconds or faster or by their [[World Athletics Ranking]] for this event.<ref name="qualification"/>
