'''Martin Fritz''' (born 24 October 1994) is an Austrian [[nordic combined]] skier.<ref name=fis>{{cite web |url=https://www.fis-ski.com/DB/general/athlete-biography.html?sectorcode=NK&competitorid=149550 |title=Martin Fritz |website=FIS-ski.com |access-date=4 December 2023 |archive-date=1 April 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230401205935/https://www.fis-ski.com/DB/general/athlete-biography.html?sectorcode=NK&competitorid=149550 |url-status=live}}</ref> He won a bronze medal in the [[FIS Nordic World Ski Championships 2023 – Team large hill/4 × 5 km|Team LH]] event at the [[FIS Nordic World Ski Championships 2023]] and competed in the [[Nordic combined at the 2022 Winter Olympics – Individual normal hill/10 km|individual normal hill]] and [[Nordic combined at the 2022 Winter Olympics – Team large hill/4 × 5 km|team LH]] events at the [[2022 Winter Olympics]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://data.fis-ski.com/pdf/2023/NK/4162/2023NK4162ROF.pdf |title=Final results: Nordic Combined -
