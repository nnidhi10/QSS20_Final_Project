The '''2015–16 FIS Cross-Country World Cup''' was the 35th official [[FIS Cross-Country World Cup|World Cup]] season in [[cross-country skiing (sport)|cross-country skiing]] for men and women. The season started on 27 November 2015 in [[Rukatunturi|Ruka]], Finland, and ended on 12 March 2016 in [[Canmore, Alberta]], Canada.

[[Therese Johaug]] set a new record for total points in one season, with 2681. [[Martin Johnsrud Sundby]] set a new men's record for total points in one season, with 2634. Sundby won 14 races during the season and surpassed [[Petter Northug]]'s record of nine victories which he achieved in both [[2009–10 FIS Cross-Country World Cup|2009–10]] and [[2012–13 FIS Cross-Country World Cup|2012–13]]. Johaug won 17 races during this season and equaled [[Marit Bjørgen]]'s record from [[2011–12 FIS Cross-Country World Cup|2011–12]]; a record Johaug herself eclipsed with 20 victories in the [[2019–20 FIS Cross-Country World Cup|2019–20]] season.
