Romanian biathlete (1941–2001)

Gheorghe Vilmoș (3 February 1941 – 16 December 2001)[1][2] was a Romanian biathlete who competed in the 1964 Winter Olympics,[3][4][5][6] 1968 Winter Olympics,[7][8][9] and 1972 Winter Olympics.[10][11]

He was born in Valea Strâmbă, Harghita County (at that time Tekerőpatak, Hungary) and was affiliated with the sports club Dinamo Brașov. He died in Brașov at age 60.[12]
