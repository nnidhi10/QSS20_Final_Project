The '''FIS Nordic Junior and U23 World Ski Championships 2017''' took place in [[Park City, Utah|Park City]], [[United States]] from 30 January to 5 February 2017.  It was the 40th [[FIS Nordic Junior World Ski Championships|Junior World Championships]] and the 12th Under-23 World Championships in [[nordic skiing]].
