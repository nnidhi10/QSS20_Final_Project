'''Dilnigar Ilhamjan''' ({{langx|ug|دىلنىگار ئىلھامجان}}; born 3 May 2001), or '''Dinigeer Yilamujiang''' ({{lang-zh|s=迪妮格尔·衣拉木江}}),<ref>{{cite web |title=YILAMUJIANG Dinigeer |url=https://www.olympics.com/en/milano-cortina-2026/results/athlete-details/51108 |work=[[Milano Cortina 2026 Olympics]] |access-date=16 February 2026}}</ref> is a Chinese [[Cross-country skiing (sport)|cross-country skier]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite news |title=Who is Dinigeer Yilamujiang, the lighter of the Olympic cauldron? |language=en-US |newspaper=Washington Post |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/sports/olympics/2022/02/04/dinigeer-yilamujiang-olympic-flame-lighting/ |access-date=4 February 2022 |issn=0190-8286 |archive-date=6 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220206052440/https://www.washingtonpost.com/sports/olympics/2022/02/04/dinigeer-yilamujiang-olympic-flame-lighting/ |url-status=live}}</ref> An ethnic [[Uyghurs|Uyghur]] from the far-northern city of [[Altay City|Altay]] in [[Xinjiang]], she is the first Chinese cross-country skier to win a medal in an [[International Ski and Snowboard Federation|FIS]] event.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |date=4 February 2022 |title=Uyghur athlete lights Olympic Cauldron as Beijing 2022 officially opens |url=https://www.insidethegames.biz/articles/1118798/beijing-2022-opening-ceremony |access-date=4 February 2022 |website=Inside the Games |archive-date=4 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220204195632/https://www.insidethegames.biz/articles/1118798/beijing-2022-opening-ceremony |url-status=live}}</ref> She made her maiden Olympic appearance during the [[2022 Winter Olympics]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=Dinigeer Yilamujiang |url=https://olympics.com/beijing-2022/olympic-games/en/results/cross-country-skiing/athlete-profile-n1058586-dinigeer-yilamujiang.htm |access-date=7 February 2022 |website=Olympics Beijing 2022 |archive-date=4 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220204152410/https://olympics.com/beijing-2022/olympic-games/en/results/cross-country-skiing/athlete-profile-n1058586-dinigeer-yilamujiang.htm |url-status=dead}}</ref> where she was one of the two last [[2022 Winter Olympics torch relay|torch bearers]].

Dilnigar also served as a representative to the 19th [[Communist Youth League of China|Communist Youth League]] Congress.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |last=青网励志李婧怡 |title=中国共产主义青年团第十九次全国代表大会代表名单 |url=https://19th.gqt.org.cn/yw/202306/t20230616_14588340.htm |access-date=3 September 2024 |website=19th.gqt.org.cn}}</ref>

==Personal life==
Her father, who taught her to ski and is still her coach,<ref>{{Cite web |last= |date=15 February 2024 |title=赛场父女兵 迪妮格尔·衣拉木江和教练父亲圆梦冬运 |url=https://finance.sina.cn/2024-02-15/detail-inaiaqvs0078304.d.html?from=wap |access-date=3 September 2024 |website=finance.sina.cn}}</ref> received a bronze medal in the 1993 national cross-country skiing competition.<ref>{{Cite news |date=5 February 2022 |title=Dinigeer Yilamujiang, a Uyghur who helped light the cauldron, made her Olympic skiing debut |newspaper=Washington Post |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/sports/olympics/2022/02/05/dinigeer-yilamujiang-cauldron-olympic-skiing/ |access-date=5 February 2022 |archive-date=5 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220205194537/https://www.washingtonpost.com/sports/olympics/2022/02/05/dinigeer-yilamujiang-cauldron-olympic-skiing/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

== Sports career ==
Originally competing as a [[Track and field|track and field athlete]], she started learning to ski when she was 12.<ref>{{cite web |last=Seow |first=Beiyi |date=5 February 2022 |title=Beijing Olympics puts young Uyghur athlete at centre of controversy |url=https://news.yahoo.com/beijing-olympics-puts-young-uyghur-072655427.html |access-date=5 February 2022 |website=news.yahoo.com |language=en-US |archive-date=5 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220205080428/https://news.yahoo.com/beijing-olympics-puts-young-uyghur-072655427.html |url-status=live}}</ref> She pursued cross-country skiing initially primarily as a hobby. In 2012 she started taking part in local competitive sports events as a long-distance runner.<ref>{{Cite web |title=中国新飞人从新疆起跑——17岁小将艾力西尔连破全国纪录的背后 |url=https://www.xjtvs.com.cn/news/2024-04/06/cms125651article.shtml |access-date=3 September 2024 |website=www.xjtvs.com.cn |archive-date=3 September 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240903114249/https://www.xjtvs.com.cn/news/2024-04/06/cms125651article.shtml |url-status=dead}}</ref> She first started skiing competitively in 2017. [[Norway|Norwegian]] coach Kristian Bjune Sveen travelled to Xinjiang to give her training, while Dilnigar also spent 3 years training in Norway herself, alongside [[Bayani Jialin]], a Chinese skier of Kazakh ethnicity.<ref>{{cite web |last=Budalen |first=Andreas |date=27 January 2022 |title=OL-treneren er en av få som ble igjen: – Håper Kina knuser Norge langt ned i støvlene |url=https://www.nrk.no/nordland/ol-treneren-er-en-av-fa-som-ble-igjen___-haper-kina-knuser-norge-langt-ned-i-stovlene-1.15827979 |access-date=5 February 2022 |website=NRK |language=nb-NO |archive-date=30 January 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220130094039/https://www.nrk.no/nordland/ol-treneren-er-en-av-fa-som-ble-igjen___-haper-kina-knuser-norge-langt-ned-i-stovlene-1.15827979 |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Hun er uigur og har trent i Norge i tre år. Fredag tente hun OL-ilden. |url=https://www.aftenposten.no/sport/i/Or13b3/hun-er-uigur-og-har-trent-i-norge-i-tre-aar-fredag-tente-hun-ol-ilden |access-date=5 February 2022 |website=www.aftenposten.no |date=4 February 2022 |language=nb |archive-date=5 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220205034010/https://www.aftenposten.no/sport/i/Or13b3/hun-er-uigur-og-har-trent-i-norge-i-tre-aar-fredag-tente-hun-ol-ilden |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last= |last2= |first2= |last3= |last4= |first4= |title=Idrettsavtalen med Kina er en skamplett for norsk idrett, skriver Andreas Selliaas. |url=https://www.morgenbladet.no/ideer/kommentar/2022/02/02/idrettsavtalen-med-kina-er-en-skamplett-for-norsk-idrett-skriver-andreas-selliaas/ |access-date=5 February 2022 |website=Morgenbladet |language=no}}</ref> She placed 2nd at the FIS China City Sprint Beijing 2019,<ref>{{Cite web |title=FIS China City Sprint Beijing 2019 |url=https://www.fis-ski.com/en/cross-country/cross-country-news-multimedia/news/2018-19/fis-china-city-sprint-beijing-2019 |access-date=4 February 2022 |website=www.fis-ski.com |language=en |archive-date=4 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220204193604/https://www.fis-ski.com/en/cross-country/cross-country-news-multimedia/news/2018-19/fis-china-city-sprint-beijing-2019 |url-status=live}}</ref> as well as 2nd at the Norwegian Norgescup at [[Konnerud]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Auklend |first=Erik Sergio |date=11 January 2019 |title=Fossesholm raskest av alle - inkludert Skistad |url=https://www.dt.no/5-57-1067833 |access-date=5 February 2022 |website=Drammens Tidende |language=no |archive-date=6 August 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240806140401/https://www.dt.no/fossesholm-raskest-av-alle-inkludert-skistad/s/5-57-1067833 |url-status=live}}</ref> This made her the first Chinese cross-country skier to win a medal in an FIS event.<ref name=":1" />

In 2019, she won a silver medal at the FIS Beijing Cross Country Skiing Points Grand Prix.<ref>{{Cite web |title=新京报 - 好新闻，无止境 |url=https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/155144415614535.html |access-date=4 February 2022 |website=www.bjnews.com.cn |archive-date=4 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220204193607/https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/155144415614535.html |url-status=live}}</ref>

=== 2022 Beijing Winter Olympics ===
{{Main|Concerns and controversies at the 2022 Winter Olympics}}
At the [[2022 Winter Olympics opening ceremony|2022 Beijing Winter Olympics opening ceremony]], she was one of the two last [[2022 Winter Olympics torch relay|torch bearers]], lighting the [[Olympic cauldron]] alongside [[Zhao Jiawen]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Beijing 2022 Opening Ceremony - Olympic cauldron lighting ends spectacular show |url=https://olympics.com/en/news/beijing-2022-opening-ceremony-dinigeer-zhao-jiawen-light-cauldron |access-date=4 February 2022 |archive-date=5 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220205231031/https://olympics.com/en/news/beijing-2022-opening-ceremony-dinigeer-zhao-jiawen-light-cauldron |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://thehill.com/blogs/blog-briefing-room/592813-china-chooses-uyghur-athlete-to-deliver-olympic-flame-amid-human |title=China chooses Uyghur athlete to help deliver Olympic flame amid human rights scrutiny |accessdate=4 February 2022 |website=The Hill |date=4 February 2022 |archive-date=4 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220204155043/https://thehill.com/blogs/blog-briefing-room/592813-china-chooses-uyghur-athlete-to-deliver-olympic-flame-amid-human |url-status=live}}</ref> She became the first [[Uyghurs|Uyghur]] and the first from [[Altay Prefecture|Altay]], the likely [[History of skiing|origin of skiing]], to light the cauldron.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Golden |first1=Andrew |title=Who is Dinigeer Yilamujiang, the lighter of the Olympic cauldron? |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/sports/olympics/2022/02/04/dinigeer-yilamujiang-olympic-flame-lighting/ |access-date=5 February 2022 |newspaper=The Washington Post |archive-date=6 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220206052440/https://www.washingtonpost.com/sports/olympics/2022/02/04/dinigeer-yilamujiang-olympic-flame-lighting/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

She competed in the [[Cross-country skiing at the 2022 Winter Olympics – Women's 15 kilometre skiathlon|women's 15km skiathlon event]] and placed 43rd.<ref>{{Cite web |title=women's 15km skiathlon event |url=https://olympics.com/beijing-2022/olympic-games/en/results/cross-country-skiing/event-schedule-women-s-7-5km-plus-7-5km-skiathlon.htm |access-date=7 February 2022 |website=Olympics Beijing 2022 |archive-date=7 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220207170725/https://olympics.com/beijing-2022/olympic-games/en/results/cross-country-skiing/event-schedule-women-s-7-5km-plus-7-5km-skiathlon.htm |url-status=dead}}</ref> After the match, she exited through a separate aisle, without passing through the mixed zone where she could be interviewed by the press.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.wsj.com/articles/a-uyghur-skier-became-the-face-of-chinas-winter-olympics-the-next-day-she-vanished-from-the-spotlight-11644157693 |title=A Uyghur Skier Became the Face of China's Winter Olympics. Unnamed sources claimed that she was physically and mentally exhausted from "having the eyes of the world on her".<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://supchina.com/2022/02/14/uyghur-torch-bearer-dropped-from-chinas-relay-team/ |title=Uyghur torch bearer dropped from China's relay team |work=SupChina |date=14 February 2022 |access-date=15 February 2022 |archive-date=15 February 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220215081842/https://supchina.com/2022/02/14/uyghur-torch-bearer-dropped-from-chinas-relay-team/ |url-status=live}}</ref> She participated in the [[Cross-country skiing at the 2022 Winter Olympics – Women's 30 kilometre freestyle|women's 30km freestyle event]] on the last day of competition.<ref>{{Cite web |date=February 2022 |title=Cross-Country Skiing- Women's 30km Mass Start Free- Results |url=https://medias2.fis-ski.com/pdf/2022/CC/2706/2022CC2706RL.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220220063904/https://medias2.fis-ski.com/pdf/2022/CC/2706/2022CC2706RL.pdf |archive-date=20 February 2022 |access-date=9 September 2024 |website=Zhangjiakou National Cross-Country Skiing Centre}}</ref>

=== Later career ===
In 2024 she won gold in cross-country skiing and silver in the women's individual sprint (traditional technique) at the Chinese National Cross-Country Skiing Championship<ref>{{Cite web |title=别眨眼！带您看2023-2024赛季全国越野滑雪冠军赛精彩瞬间_比赛_格尔·_付浩 |url=https://www.sohu.com/a/803799783_121449582 |access-date=3 September 2024 |website=www.sohu.com}}</ref> as well as a silver medal at the 2024 [[National Winter Games of China|China National Winter Games]] double pursuit.<ref>{{Cite web |title=“十四冬”凉城赛区开赛 新疆队选手迪妮格尔·衣拉木江获银牌 -天山网 - 新疆新闻门户 |url=https://www.ts.cn/xwzx/ymkxj/202402/t20240215_19202816.shtml |access-date=3 September 2024 |website=www.ts.cn}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=第十四届全国冬季运动会 |url=https://www.sport.gov.cn/n4/n27461473/n27464526/c27485458/content.html |access-date=3 September 2024 |website=www.sport.gov.cn}}</ref>
She placed 36th in the [[ Cross-country skiing at the 2026 Winter Olympics – Women's 50 kilometre classical|50 km classical]] race at the [[2026 Winter Olympics]].<ref>[https://www.reuters.com/sports/crosscountry-swedens-andersson-wins-50km-classic-race-2026-02-22/ Sweden's Andersson claims decisive victory in cross-country 50km classic race] [[Reuters]]. Retrieved 22 February 2026.</ref>

== Political activities ==
In 2022 she was listed as an outstanding member of the [[Communist Youth League of China|Communist Youth League]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=全国优秀共青团员名单-中青在线 |url=https://zqb.cyol.com/html/2022-04/15/nw.D110000zgqnb_20220415_1-06.htm |access-date=3 September 2024 |website=zqb.cyol.com}}</ref> She was a representative to the 19th Youth League Congress for Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region.<ref name=":2" /><ref>{{Cite web |title=团代会分组会上的青年代表风采_新闻频道__中国青年网 |url=https://picture.youth.cn/qtdb/202306/t20230619_14595150_6.htm |access-date=17 February 2025 |website=picture.youth.cn}}</ref>
