'''Natalya Mikhaylovna Terentyeva''' ({{langx|ru|Наталья Михайловна Терентьева}}, née Nepryaeva, born 6 September 1995) is a Russian [[Cross-country skiing (sport)|cross-country skier]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://infosport.ru/person/lijnie-gonki/nepryaeva-natalya-mihailovna|title=Российские спортсмены и специалисты|publisher=Спортивная Россия|language=Russian|accessdate=10 February 2018}}</ref> She participated in the [[2018 Winter Olympics]] as part of the [[Olympic Athletes from Russia at the 2018 Winter Olympics|Olympic Athletes from Russia team]] and [[2022 Winter Olympics]] as part of the [[Russian Olympic Committee athletes at the 2022 Winter Olympics|Russian Olympic Committee team]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympic.org/pyeongchang-2018/results/en/cross-country-skiing/results-ladies-7-5km--plus--7-5km-skiathlon-fnl-000100-.htm|title=Results – Ladies' 7.5km + 7.5km Skiathlon|publisher=2018 Winter Games official site|accessdate=10 February 2018}}</ref> winning a total of four medals, including a gold in the [[Cross-country skiing at the 2022 Winter Olympics – Women's 4 × 5 kilometre relay|4 × 5 km relay]].

==Career==
Nepryayeva was first included to the Russian national team in 2015.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tverlife.ru/news/tverskaya-lyzhnitsa-natalya-nepryaeva-vtoroe-i-trete-mesta-eto-neudacha-.html|title=Тверская лыжница Наталья Непряева: второе и третье места – это неудача|publisher=Тверская жизнь|language=Russian|accessdate=10 February 2018|archive-date=16 December 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181216032220/https://tverlife.ru/news/tverskaya-lyzhnitsa-natalya-nepryaeva-vtoroe-i-trete-mesta-eto-neudacha-.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> Her breakthrough came in [[Cross-country skiing at the 2018 Winter Olympics|2018]] when she won bronze as part of the Russian relay team. 

In the [[2018–19 FIS Cross-Country World Cup|2018–19 season]], Nepryayeva became the first Russian female skier to finish in the top 3 of the [[2018–19 Tour de Ski|Tour de Ski]].<ref>[https://www.championat.com/other/article-3642323-lyzhnye-gonki-tur-de-ski-2019-neprjaeva--vtoraja-v-obschem-zachjote.html Это фантастика! Российская лыжница вошла в историю «Тур де Ски»]</ref> At the [[2019 FIS Nordic World Ski Championships]], Nepryayeva became the first female Russian skier since [[FIS Nordic World Ski Championships 2007|2007]] to medal in [[skiathlon]] (then known as double pursuit).<ref>[https://www.championat.com/other/article-3685611-chempionat-mira-po-lyzhnym-gonkam-2019-neprjaeva-zavoevala-bronzu-v-skiatlone.html Нас не ждали, а мы пришли. Есть первая женская медаль России на ЧМ по лыжам!]</ref> Overall, she won three medals at the World Championships in 2019 and 2021.

Nepryayeva became the first Russian female skier to win the [[2021–22 Tour de Ski|Tour de Ski]], doing so during the [[2021–22 FIS Cross-Country World Cup|2021–22 World Cup]] season.<ref>[https://tass.com/sport/1383803 Natalia Nepryaeva becomes first Russian to win Tour de Ski’s overall standings ]</ref> Furthermore, she won the World Cup, despite being excluded from the last two stages due to sanctions related to the [[Russian invasion of Ukraine]]. She became the first Russian female skier to do so since [[Yuliya Chepalova]] in the 2000–01 season.
