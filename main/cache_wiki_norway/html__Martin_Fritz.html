Martin Fritz (born 24 October 1994) is an Austrian nordic combined skier.[2] He won a bronze medal in the Team LH event at the FIS Nordic World Ski Championships 2023 and competed in the individual normal hill and team LH events at the 2022 Winter Olympics.[3][4][5][1]
