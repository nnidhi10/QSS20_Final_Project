Zachary Plavsic (Serbian: Захари Плавишић; born January 15, 1983) is a Canadian windsurfer of Serbian background. Plavsic was born and raised in the city of Vancouver, BC, Canada.[1] He has represented Canada in two Olympic Games. In the 2008 Summer Olympics in Beijing, China, Plavsic finished 23rd.[2] In the 2012 Summer Olympics in London, United Kingdom, Plavsic placed in 8th.[3] In the 2011 ISAF Sailing World Cup, Plavsic finished Second and at the 2011 ISAF Sailing World Championships, Plavsic finished in 12th spot.[4]
