'''Zachary Plavsic''' ({{langx|sr|Захари Плавишић}}; born January 15, 1983) is a Canadian [[Windsurfing|windsurfer]] of [[Serbs|Serbian]] background. Plavsic was born and raised in the city of Vancouver, BC, Canada.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ebritic.com/?p=202598&lang=SR|title=Olympic Serbs in the Diaspora|access-date=2012-10-05|archive-date=2016-03-04|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304043037/http://www.ebritic.com/?p=202598&lang=SR|url-status=dead}}</ref> He has represented Canada in two [[Olympic Games]]. In the [[2008 Summer Olympics]] in [[Beijing, China]], Plavsic finished 23rd.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://olympic.ca/team-canada/zachary-plavsic#news|title = Zachary Plavsic}}</ref> In the [[2012 Summer Olympics]] in [[London, United Kingdom]], Plavsic placed in 8th.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.london2012.com/sailing/event/rs-x-men/phase=sam102101/index.html|title=London 2012 RSX - Windsurfer men Results - Olympic sailing|access-date=2012-08-19|archive-date=2012-08-05|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120805113740/http://www.london2012.com/sailing/event/rs-x-men/phase=sam102101/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> In the [[2011 ISAF Sailing World Cup]], Plavsic finished Second and at the [[2011 ISAF Sailing World Championships]], Plavsic finished in 12th spot.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://olympic.ca/team-canada/zachary-plavsic#news|title = Zachary Plavsic}}</ref>
