'''Annette Duetz''' (born 29 June 1993 in [[Zeddam]])<ref>{{cite web |url=http://kernploeg.watersporters.nl/delta-lloyd-talentploeg/49erfx/annette-duetz/ |title=Annette Duetz |publisher=Wateesportverbond |language=Dutch |accessdate=4 May 2016}}</ref> is a [[sailing (sport)|sailor]] from the [[Netherlands]].  Together with [[Annemiek Bekkering]], she is the bronze medalist of the 2020 Summer Olympics in [[Sailing at the 2020 Summer Olympics – 49er FX|Skiff – 49er FX]]. She is, together with [[Odile van Aanholt]], the gold medalist of the [[2024 Paris Olympics]] in the [[Sailing at the 2024 Summer Olympics – 49er FX|woman's 49er FX]].

Bekkering and Duetz qualified for the [[2016 Summer Olympics]] in [[Sailing at the 2016 Summer Olympics – 49er FX|Skiff – 49er FX]]. They earlier lost the national selection to [[Nina Keizer]] en [[Claire Blom]], but Keizer and Blom failed to fulfill the qualification criteria of NOC/NSF.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://nos.nl/artikel/2102398-olympisch-ticket-zeilduo-bekkering-duetz.html |title=Olympisch ticket zeilduo Bekkering/Duetz |date=30 April 2016 |publisher=NOS |language=Dutch |accessdate=4 May 2016}}</ref> At the Olympics, Bekkering and Duetz finished in the seventh position.<ref>{{cite web |title=Rio 2016 Sailing 49er FX Women women Results |url=https://olympics.com/en/olympic-games/rio-2016/results/sailing/49er-fx-women-women |work=Rio 2016 Olympics |publisher=[[Rio 2016 Organising Committee for the Olympic and Paralympic Games]] |access-date=22 November 2021 |archive-date=22 November 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211122062015/https://olympics.com/en/olympic-games/rio-2016/results/sailing/49er-fx-women-women |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Since 2011, Duetz studied Applied Physics at [[Delft University of Technology]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://delta.tudelft.nl/artikel/annette-duetz-definitief-naar-rio/31581 |title=Annette Duetz definitief naar Rio |date=2 May 2016 |work=Delta |publisher=Delft University of Technology |language=Dutch |access-date=4 May 2016}}</ref>
