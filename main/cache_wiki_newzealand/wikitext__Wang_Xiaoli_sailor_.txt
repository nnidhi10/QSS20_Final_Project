'''Xiaoli Wang''' (12 May 1982, Binzhou) is a Chinese sports sailor.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/wa/wang-xiaoli-1.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200418055542/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/wa/wang-xiaoli-1.html|url-status=dead|archive-date=2020-04-18|title=Wang Xiaoli Bio, Stats, and Results|website=Olympics at Sports-Reference.com|language=en|access-date=2018-04-18}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.london2012.com/athlete/wang-xiaoli-1067102/ |title=Xiaoli Wang |work=[[2012 Summer Olympics]] |accessdate=12 October 2012 |archive-date=30 October 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121030174249/http://www.london2012.com/athlete/wang-xiaoli-1067102/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> At the [[2012 Summer Olympics]], she competed in the [[Sailing at the 2012 Summer Olympics – Women's 470|Women's 470 class]].  She and team-mate [[Huang Xufeng]] finished in 11th place.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.olympic.org/olympic-results/london-2012/sailing/470-two-person-dinghy-w|title = London 2012 470 - Two Person Dinghy women Results - Olympic sailing}}</ref>  At the 2016 Olympics, she teamed with [[Huang Lizhu]], finishing in 16th place.<ref name=":0" />

At the 2013 470 World Championships, Wang and Huang Xufeng became the first Chinese team to win a medal at the 470 World Championships, winning a bronze medal.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web|url=http://www.sailing.org/news/1903.php#.WtepdIjwbIU|title=Mat Belcher Makes 470 History As Olympic Champions Win In La Rochelle|website=sailing.org|language=en|access-date=2018-04-18}}</ref>  The team were coached by [[Kevin Burnham]] and Nick Drougas''.''<ref name=":1" />
